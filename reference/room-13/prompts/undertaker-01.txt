Image 1 shows two figures from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is the undertaker, cut from image 2 at Thad's size. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his face is a handful of blocks; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: the undertaker's shop at night. The man in the white apron planing a board at the table is the undertaker. I need the undertaker drawn on his own, whole, as a sheet of five poses, in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE.

THE CHARACTER -- the undertaker, as image 2 shows him: a man in his fifties, lean, clean-shaven, grey hair cut short, white shirt with the sleeves rolled, a clean white apron, dark trousers, plain black shoes, a carpenter's hands. Calm, unhurried, completely unbothered by anything. A little shorter than Thad.

THE SHEET -- one image, five poses of this same man, side by side, left to right, each FULL LENGTH from head to shoes:
1. REST: standing, facing the viewer and turned slightly to the left of the picture, both hands resting together in front of him at waist height, as if on a table top.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his shoulders are very slightly raised. Nothing else changes.
3. PLANING: as pose 1, but both hands hold a carpenter's wooden plane at waist height, pushing it forward, his head bowed over the work.
4. PUTTING IT DOWN: as pose 1, but he is setting the plane down at waist height with his right hand and looking up, without hurry.
5. WAITING: as pose 1, hands folded, looking straight out at the viewer, face expressionless.

STYLE: every pose about 80 big pixels tall from head to shoes, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; the face as simple as Thad's -- a few blocks, dark blocks for the eyes.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no shadow, no table, nothing else in the picture.
- He stands on his own and touches nothing (the plane is held in his hands).
- All five poses the same size, with his shoes on the same baseline, each about two-thirds the height of the picture.
- Clear magenta space between poses. No pose touches or overlaps another.
- Between poses, ONLY the change described happens. Same clothes, same colours, same face everywhere else.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur around him.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
