THE ROOM: the undertaker's shop in a gold-rush town, 1850s, at night. Scrubbed bone-white and pale pine, near-monochrome on purpose; clean, plain, patient; nothing wasted. Image 5 is a saloon in the same town at the same hour, for the world and the night light only.
- Far left: a plain street door with no bell. Beside it a small window onto the dark lane; under it a plain pine waiting bench.
- Left of middle: a small painted price board on the wall; below it one good black suit hanging on a peg, with two more in other sizes behind it.
- Middle: a doorway to the back room whose frame is marked with pencil heights, like a family kitchen; then pine coffins stacked by size against the wall, from big to very many small ones.
- Right of middle: a long scrubbed oak table, the room's one working surface, with an oil lamp hanging over it. On a shelf above it, a vase of wax lilies and a glass jar of teeth.
- Right: a black hearse harness hanging on a peg; a wall rack of carpenter's tools; a small stand with an open ledger of accounts.
- Floor: scrubbed pine boards.
- Light: the lamp over the table, warm but pale; the corners dimmer. No daylight.

THE PEOPLE (this picture is a reference painting, so people are allowed in it):
- The hero from image 4, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size, facing right toward the table.
- THE UNDERTAKER, standing behind the oak table, seen from the waist up over it, planing a pine board with a carpenter's plane, not looking up: a man in his fifties, lean, clean-shaven, grey hair cut short, shirt sleeves rolled, a clean white apron, a carpenter's hands. Calm and completely unbothered. The size of a standing man at that depth.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as images 2, 3 and 6, and the same hand as the hero: the SAME BIG SQUARE PIXELS as the hero -- the undertaker drawn on exactly the hero's coarse pixel grid, no finer -- flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes. This room has its own colours: bone-white, pale pine, a little black; warm pale lamplight. Nothing near black where the hero stands.
