Image 1 is a finished background from my pixel-art adventure game, with five people in it: two men standing on the floor in the middle and three men seated behind the table.

Remove all five people. Paint what is behind each of them, exactly as the room would look with nobody in it: the floorboards and the burnt line on the floor, the table's edge and legs, the three empty chairs behind the table, the wall, the lamplight on the floor.

Change nothing else. Same framing, same camera, same pixel grid, same colours, same light, same objects in the same places: the door, the slate, the tin, the lamp, the window, the table and what is on it, every chair. No people, no shadows of people, no text.
