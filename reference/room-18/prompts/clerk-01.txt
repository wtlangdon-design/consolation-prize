Image 1 shows, side by side at the same scale, Thad, the hero of my pixel-art adventure game (left: the style standard), and the hotel clerk's head and shoulders (right), cut from image 2. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his face is a handful of blocks; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: the hotel lobby at night. The man behind the desk reading the register is the hotel clerk. I need him drawn on his own, WHOLE, as a sheet of five poses, in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE.

THE CHARACTER -- the hotel clerk, as image 2 shows him: a neat, fussy man of about forty, stout, dark hair parted in the centre and oiled flat, a dark moustache, small round spectacles, a white shirt with black sleeve garters, a buttoned dark waistcoat, a red bow tie, dark trousers. Proprietorial about a building he does not own; not rude -- administering.

THE SHEET -- one image, five poses of this same man, side by side, left to right, each FULL LENGTH from head to shoes, standing, facing the viewer:
1. REST: both hands resting flat in front of him at waist height, as if on a desk top, looking down as if reading.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his shoulders very slightly raised. Nothing else changes.
3. WRITING: as pose 1, but one hand holding a pen at waist height, writing, his head bent.
4. LOOKING UP: as pose 1, but his head raised, looking straight out over his spectacles, eyebrows up.
5. TURNING THE BOOK: as pose 1, but both hands turning a large book round at waist height to face the viewer.

STYLE: every pose about 75 big pixels tall from head to shoes, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; the face as simple as Thad's -- a few blocks, dark blocks for the eyes.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no shadow, no desk, nothing else in the picture except the pen in pose 3 and the book in pose 5.
- All five poses the same size, shoes on the same baseline, each about two-thirds the height of the picture.
- Clear magenta space between poses. No pose touches or overlaps another.
- Between poses, ONLY the change described happens. Same clothes, same colours, same face everywhere else.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur around him.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
