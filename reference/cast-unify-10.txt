Image 1 is from the locked cast sheet of my pixel-art adventure game, a comedy in an 1850s frontier gold town: left to right, the hero (THE STYLE AND THE SCALE), then three characters, A, B and C. These designs are FINAL. Draw each of A, B and C EXACTLY as they are in Image 1 -- the same face, hair, age, build, clothes and colours, the same size of square pixel, the same amount of detail, the same soft shading and dark outline -- only in new poses.

Each of them stands behind a counter, and THE COUNTER AND EVERYTHING ON IT ARE ALREADY PAINTED IN THE GAME'S BACKGROUND. So draw NO counter, NO book, NO ledger, NO paper, NO desk things of any kind. Draw only the person, from the waist up:
- the body is cut off in ONE straight horizontal line across the waist, where the counter's back edge hides it;
- the forearms come FORWARD over the counter top, toward the viewer, so the hands lie a little BELOW that waist line, resting on the counter top (which is not drawn -- only magenta there);
- between and around the hands, below the waist line, there is nothing but magenta.

THE SHEET: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. Three rows, one character per row, each shown FOUR times side by side, every figure in a row the same size, separated by clear magenta space:

- Row 3, C, the company clerk (pale blond hair parted in the middle, a thin moustache, high collar, dark waistcoat, sleeve garters): 1. hands resting flat on the counter; 2. writing in a small notebook that he holds up in his other hand; 3. holding up a pocket watch and reading it; 4. looking up, hands resting.

RULES: only the pen, the stamp, the notebook and the watch, in the hands where named. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta, no glow, no blur, no shadows, no text, no numbers, no border.
