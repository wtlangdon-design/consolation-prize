THE SHEET, left to right, on flat pure magenta (#FF00FF):

The townsman is the SAME man and the ladder the SAME ladder in poses 2-4: only his arm and head change. A man the hero's height: if he stood on the ground he would be as tall as the hero. The ladder's feet in poses 2-4 sit on the same ground line as the hero's feet.

RULES: no sign, no text, no lettering, no background, no ground, no shadows, no border. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta.
