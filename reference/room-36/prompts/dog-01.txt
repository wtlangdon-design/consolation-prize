2. PAW: the same, one front paw stretched up a little further, as a sleeping dog does.
3. TAIL: the same as frame 1, his tail flopped over to the other side.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No ground, no shadow, nothing else.

- Between frames ONLY the change described happens. Same dog everywhere.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
