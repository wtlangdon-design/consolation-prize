Paint this place as a finished game background with the person below in it, keeping the camera, the layout and every block where image 1 puts it. THE PLACE: the head of the gulch above the gold camp of image 2 -- the same river, further up -- frontier West, 1850s, on a clear DAY.
- The ravine walls close in on both sides and fill the top, with a narrow strip of sky.
- At the back, in the middle: a LITTLE WATERFALL where the gulch ends, no taller than a man, falling over rock, with more rock behind it and above it. Nothing beyond.
- THE RIVER runs down from the falls, shallow and brown, across the middle and out at the FRONT LEFT corner.
- Beside the river, left of centre: a SLUICE, a long wooden trough on legs, HALF BUILT -- some boards missing, the end unfinished -- with one plain board nailed across it, with NO writing on it.
- The rest of the ground, the middle and the right: THE BEST GROUND, flat gravel with small flecks of gold showing in it.
- On that gravel, right of centre: TWO LINES OF WOODEN STAKES driven into the ground, CROSSING each other in an X, and on one stake of each line a small paper notice, with NO writing on it.
- At the back right: a small canvas tent pitched right where the two lines of stakes run.

THE PEOPLE:
- The hero from image 3, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size.
- Standing on the gravel among the stakes, right of centre: THE MAN UPSTREAM, a gold miner alone, standing guard over the gravel with a shovel planted beside him, scowling down the river. About as tall as the hero would be standing there.
Both drawn exactly as coarsely as the hero.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as image 2, and the same hand as the hero: the SAME BIG SQUARE PIXELS as the hero, flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes. Clear daylight. The water and the gravel are middle tones, never bright white.

RULES: no readable text, letters or numbers anywhere -- the board and the notices are blank. Nobody else. No border, no frame around the picture, no caption.
