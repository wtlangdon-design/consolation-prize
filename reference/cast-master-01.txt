I am making the cast of my pixel-art adventure game, a comedy set in an 1850s frontier gold town at night. I need EVERY character drawn TOGETHER, on ONE sheet, by ONE artist, so they all belong to the same game.

Image 1 is THE ART STYLE: the hero, the night watchman and the swindler. Match exactly their level of pixelation (the same size of square pixels for a figure this tall), their amount of detail, their soft simple shading with two or three tones per colour, their muted, slightly dusty palette, their dark outlines and their realistic, very slightly caricatured proportions. Use LESS detail rather than more.
