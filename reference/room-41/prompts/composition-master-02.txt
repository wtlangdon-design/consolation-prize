Image 1 is the layout for one place in my pixel-art adventure game: grey blocks for the room and the things in it, and the hero (the man in the long coat) standing on the floor. Image 2 is a first painting of this same room: KEEP ITS LOOK -- the new plank walls, the cool moonlit night, the desks, the blackboard, the stove, the window onto the town's far-off lights -- but it got three things wrong, and this painting must fix them:
- it drew SIXTEEN desks. Draw EXACTLY FOURTEEN small school desks, in TWO ROWS OF SEVEN, where image 1's fourteen small blocks are;
- it drew FIVE coat hooks. Draw a long wooden rail on the wall beside the door with EXACTLY FOURTEEN small coat hooks on it, evenly spaced, where image 1's long thin block is;
- it painted too short a picture. Paint the WHOLE middle band of image 1, from its top edge to its bottom edge and from side to side; leave only the light grey strips above and below it flat light grey.

THE PLACE: a new one-room schoolhouse in a frontier gold-mining town, 1850s, at NIGHT -- well built, clean, and never once used. No lamp is lit: cool moonlight comes through the window.
- At the far left: the schoolhouse door, shut, on new hinges; beside it the rail of fourteen empty coat hooks.
- Then one clean glass window; through it, far off, the scattered warm lights of the town.
- Centre: the fourteen small hand-made wooden desks in two rows of seven, each with a lid and an inkwell, all empty.
- Back right: a washed blank blackboard with a small unopened box of chalk on its ledge; in front of it the teacher's desk with a polished brass handbell on it.
- Far right: a small iron school stove, blacked and cold, kindling laid in it, a kettle on top, one cup beside it, a full coal scuttle.

THE PEOPLE: only the hero from image 3, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size. Nobody else.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as images 2 and 4, and the same hand as the hero: the SAME BIG SQUARE PIXELS as the hero, flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes. Cool and still; the room readable, the floor where the hero stands not black.

RULES: no readable text, letters or numbers anywhere -- the blackboard is blank. No border, no frame around the picture, no caption.
