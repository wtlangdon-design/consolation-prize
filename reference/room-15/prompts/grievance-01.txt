Image 1 shows two figures from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is Grievance, a mule, cut from image 2 as she looks out over her stall door. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: the livery stable at night. The mule looking out over the half door of the middle stall is Grievance. I need her drawn on her own, WHOLE, as a sheet of five poses, in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE.

THE CHARACTER -- Grievance, as image 2 shows her: a mule the colour of a wet saddle, dark brown, darker mane, long ears, a pale muzzle; solid and in no hurry; an expression of settled disapproval. She stands FACING THE VIEWER, seen from the front and a little from her left, head up, as she does looking out over her door in image 2.

THE SHEET -- one image, five poses of this same mule, side by side, left to right, each showing her WHOLE, head to hooves:
1. REST: standing, facing the viewer, head up, both ears up, looking straight out.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except her nostrils slightly flared and her neck very slightly raised. Nothing else changes.
3. EARS: as pose 1, but both ears swivelled round to point to the LEFT of the picture, listening; her head has not moved.
4. LOOK: as pose 1, but her whole head turned to look to the LEFT of the picture, long and level, with disapproval.
5. SNORT: as pose 1, but her head tossed slightly up and her nostrils blowing out a breath.

STYLE: each pose about 70 big pixels tall from ear tips to hooves, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; her eyes a few dark blocks.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no shadow, no stall, no door, nothing else in the picture.
- All five poses the same size, hooves on the same baseline, each about two-thirds the height of the picture.
- Clear magenta space between poses. No pose touches or overlaps another.
- Between poses, ONLY the change described happens. Same colours, same markings everywhere else.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur around her.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
