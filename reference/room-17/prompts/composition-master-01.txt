- Far left: the plain front door, standing slightly open, never locked.
- Beside the door: a painted board of services and prices; below it a small stand with a brass collection plate on it.
- Left: a paper subscription list pinned to the wall.
- Above the door end: a square hatch in the ceiling up into the belfry, the underside of a bell visible through it; a long bell rope hanging down from it.
- Back wall, middle: two tall plain windows of ordinary glass, dark night outside.
- Middle: rows of plain wooden pews; a hymnal lying open on the end of one.
- Right of middle: a big iron stove, too big for the room, glowing.
- Right: a raised wooden pulpit with a reading desk.
- Far right: the meeting end, a long plain table with chairs.
- Light: oil lamps on the walls and the stove; warm, the corners dim.
