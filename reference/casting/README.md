# Casting reference — Room 1

**`room-01-casting-master.png`** — the casting sheet. Every character in the room, in the room, in one generation, so they share a style by construction. **Never used as an asset.** Its only job is to fix the style and the cast once, so each character can be requested alone by *pointing at them in it*.

Method and rules: `docs/38-character-pipeline.md`.

Two attempts at describing a character in words instead came back photographic and had to be thrown away. Pointing worked first try.

**`room-01-plate-approved.png`** — the approved Room 1 background, 1870 × 841. Target play area is 1920 × 864; the 2.7% upscale is accepted rather than regenerating an approved plate (issue Q6).

**`hob-profile-source.png`** — Hob, requested off the casting sheet, on green. The input to `tools/rig/character.py`. Prefer **magenta** for future characters in dark clothing; green sat too close to his coat's shadows and cost ~19,000 despilled pixels.

*The master carries lettering on the sign and is 16:9 rather than the plate's 2.22:1. Neither matters for casting.*
