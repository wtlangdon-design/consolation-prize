STYLE: exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; the same soft shading and dark outline. Not sharp, not detailed.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No bell, no steps, no floor, no shadow.
- All five poses the same size, boots on the same baseline, each about two thirds of the height of the picture.
- Clear magenta space between poses. No pose touches or overlaps another.
- Between poses, ONLY the change described happens.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
