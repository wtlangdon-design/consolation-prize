THE SHEET: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. TWO rows of SIX full figures, each man ALONE, all at that same three-quarter angle, in the order below; every figure the SAME height and scale, as large as fits, feet on one line in each row, separated by clear magenta space.
- Row 1: the FIRST miner (blue coat, blue-grey bowler, a pickaxe).
- Row 2: the SECOND miner (brown coat and waistcoat, brown hat, clean-shaven, a long-handled shovel).

THE SIX FRAMES OF EACH ROW ARE SIX DIFFERENT POSES -- the feet come TOGETHER twice in the cycle, never a wide stride held. His NEAR leg (nearer the viewer) is lighter; his FAR leg darker:
1. NEAR leg forward, heel down; far leg stretched behind, on its toe. Widest stride.
2. weight onto the near leg, which straightens under him; the far foot comes off the ground, knee a little bent.
3. PASSING: the far leg swings forward past the near leg, the legs close together.
4. FAR leg forward, heel down; the near leg stretched behind, on its toe. Widest stride.
5. weight onto the far leg; the near foot comes off the ground, knee a little bent.
6. PASSING: the near leg swings forward past the far leg, the legs close together.
The steps are along his OWN heading -- his feet, knees and hips point the way he walks, exactly the same direction as his body and head.

HIS TOOL: over his shoulder, ONE HAND GRIPPING ITS HANDLE IN ALL SIX FRAMES -- the same tool, at the same angle, in every frame. His free arm swings a little opposite to his legs.

RULES: one tool per man, nothing else in his hands. No ground, no shadows, no text, no numbers, no border. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta.
