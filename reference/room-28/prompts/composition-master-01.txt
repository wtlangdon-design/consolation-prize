THE PLACE: a Chinese laundry in a frontier gold-mining district, 1850s, at NIGHT, still working -- the most orderly, competent business in town, and the cleanest room. Plank walls, a plank floor, steam hanging in the air. Lit warmly by the fires under the boilers and one oil lamp; the corners darker. Same night as image 2, which is another room in the same game.
- Left, high on the back wall: one small window, fogged with steam, a little clear circle wiped in it.
- Left, below it: three big copper boilers in a row, each over its own small fire, steaming.
- Beside them: a stoneware starch crock.
- Middle: a wooden mangle with a crank; behind it, a small iron stove with flat irons on it, and an ironing board in front of the stove.
- High on the wall above: a rail of hanging paper laundry tickets.
- Back, centre-right: a doorway to a back room, closed off by a clean curtain.
- Right of it: a counter with an open ledger, a second smaller book on a shelf above it, a set of brass scales and a tin cash box; starched white collars hanging in a row above the counter.
- By the right wall: a wooden chair facing out of the door, a cup on the floor beside it, and a mending basket.
- Far right: the door standing open on the night: a stepped stone path climbing a dark ridge outside, and lines of white shirts drying in the wind.
- At the far left edge: the way out back to the road.
