Image 1 shows two figures from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is Ah-Lam Sheng at his ironing board, roughly cut from image 2. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his face is a handful of blocks; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: the laundry at night, lit by the boiler fires. The man ironing in the middle is the one I need, drawn on his own WITH HIS IRONING BOARD in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE, as a sheet of eight frames in two rows.

THE CHARACTER -- Ah-Lam Sheng as image 2 shows him: a Chinese laundryman and banker of about fifty, slight and upright, short dark hair, a plain dark tunic with cloth buttons, sleeves rolled, dark trousers, black shoes. Precise, economical, dry. He stands BEHIND a wooden ironing board on folding legs, a white shirt laid on it, a heavy flat iron in his right hand. Three-quarter view, FACING LEFT. Every frame shows him and the board WHOLE, head to shoes and board legs.

TOP ROW:
1. REST: ironing as in image 2, the iron resting on the shirt, eyes on his work.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his chest very slightly fuller. Nothing else changes.
3. LOOK: as pose 1, but his head raised, looking to the LEFT of the picture at someone, unhurried.
4. STROKE: the iron pushed along the shirt to the LEFT, his arm extended.

BOTTOM ROW:
5. RETURN: the iron drawn back to the RIGHT along the shirt.
6. INSPECT: the iron lifted near his face, checking its heat, the shirt flat on the board.
7. FOLD: both hands folding the shirt over on itself, the iron set down at the board's end.
8. TALK: as pose 3, looking LEFT, one hand lifted a little from the board as he makes a dry point.

STYLE: about 80 big pixels tall from head to shoes, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; the face as simple as Thad's -- a few blocks, dark blocks for the eyes.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no stove, no shadow, nothing else in the picture.
- Every frame is one piece -- the man, the board, the shirt and the iron -- and does not touch any other frame.
- All frames the same size, feet and board legs on the same baseline within each row; clear magenta space between frames and between the rows.
- Between frames, ONLY the change described happens. Same man, same board, same colours.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
