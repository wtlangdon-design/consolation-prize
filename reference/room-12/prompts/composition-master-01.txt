THE ROOM: the general store of a gold-rush town, 1850s, at night: the Mercantile. Crowded, warm, everything for sale; broken up by bolts of fabric, tin cans and printed paper labels. Nobody behind the counter -- the storekeeper is in the back room. Image 5 is a saloon on the same street at the same hour, for the world and the night light only.
- Far left: the street door. Beside it, high on the wall, pairs of boots tied together by the laces and hung on a nail; below them a rack of pickaxes, one with a loose head; a barrel of shovels with price tags on strings.
- Left of middle: a tower of tin gold pans nested one inside the next up toward the ceiling; a big painted price board on the wall; a pot-bellied iron stove with a ring of old chairs around it and a coffee pot on top; beside the stove a wooden crate of dynamite with its lid open and a newspaper over the sticks.
- Middle: a table stacked with bolts of calico, the outer folds faded.
- Right: a long scarred wooden counter. On it, from left to right: a brass weighing scale with a dish of lead shot; a big open credit ledger; a very thick mail-order catalogue lying open, with five paper slips sticking up out of it as bookmarks; a loose order form beside it; and at the counter's end a huge glass jar of pickled eggs with a wooden spoon on a string.
- Behind the counter, shelves to the ceiling: tinned goods with printed labels; a row of brown medicine bottles of patent tonic with a bearded doctor on the labels; a dozen unsold scales still wrapped in yellowed paper; cans of lamp oil. A curtained doorway to the back room.
- In front of the counter's end: a keg of nails.
- Floor: worn boards.
- Light: an oil lamp hanging over the counter and the stove's glow; the corners dimmer. No daylight.

THE HERO from image 4, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size, facing right toward the counter.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as images 2, 3 and 6: flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes, a visible pixel grid. This room has its own colours: warm wood and lamplight, broken up by faded calico blue, tin silver and bright printed labels. Nothing near black where the hero stands.

RULES: no readable text, letters or numbers anywhere -- the labels, the price board, the ledger, the catalogue and the order form carry marks, never words. No border, no frame around the picture, no caption.
