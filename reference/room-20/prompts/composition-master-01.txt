Paint this place as a finished game background with the hero in it, keeping the camera, the layout and every block where image 1 puts it. THE PLACE: the back alley behind the main street of a gold-rush town, 1850s, at night. The backs of the buildings, where the painted false fronts stop pretending: bare boards, bare posts, mud.
- Far left: a narrow gap between two buildings, through which the lamplit main street glows warm.
- Left: a row of plain back doors in the backs of the buildings, each a different door; one is propped open with a brick, and warm lamplight falls out of it across the alley onto a stack of empty wooden shipping crates.
- On the top crate, in that light, sits a small tabby cat.
- Middle: a rain barrel under a tin downspout.
- High across the left half of the alley, a wash line strung from wall to wall, with one shirt and a set of long underwear hanging on it.
- Right half: the back of one long, low, one-story building with no windows at all; above its roof, the timber bracing that holds up the tall false front of its other side, seen from behind against the night sky. In its back wall, a plain side door with no handle. Beside the door, a low heap of grey ash with scraps of burnt paper in it. At the right, a neat pyramid of empty flour barrels, stacked true and square.
- Light: the open back door's warm spill and the glow from the street gap; the rest moonlit and dim.
