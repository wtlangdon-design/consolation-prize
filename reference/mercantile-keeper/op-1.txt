Image 1 is a canvas on magenta from my pixel-art adventure game, set in an 1850s gold-rush town. The figure on the left is the hero, Thad, standing: he shows the size of a standing man and the game's standard -- flat, soft shading, low texture. Keep him exactly as he is.

Image 2 is the general store (the Mercantile) at game size, with Thad standing at its counter. The new man stands BEHIND that counter, between the open ledger on the counter and the big pickle jar, in front of the shelves of tins and bottles. This is the light he stands in: warm, dim lamplight from the lantern above the shelves behind him. Image 3 is the saloon's barman from the same game, enlarged: he is THE DRAWING STYLE TO MATCH -- big simple shapes, two or three tones per material, soft broad shading, no fine wrinkles, no crisp highlights, no hard dark lines inside the figure, warm and dim, not bright and not white.

On the magenta to the right of Thad, draw FOUR full-length standing figures of ONE new man, in a row, each separated by clear magenta, their feet on the same line as Thad's, each a little shorter than Thad (about 90% of his height): the store's storekeeper. A lean, cheerful man of about forty-five, clean-shaven, thinning hair combed flat, a PENCIL TUCKED BEHIND EACH EAR (one behind the left ear, one behind the right ear, both visible), shirt sleeves held up by sleeve garters on his upper arms, a dark waistcoat, a brown store apron, dark trousers. Patient and pleased with himself. His colours are the store's under that lamplight: the shirt a warm, dim cream (NOT white), the apron a soiled brown, the waistcoat dark; overall no brighter than Thad. He faces us, turned only slightly toward the viewer's left, so both ears and both pencils show. A NARROW figure: elbows in at his sides, his hands close together in front of him at the height of his belt (that is where the counter top will be). He holds no book.
1. standing, both hands resting flat and close together at belt height (on the counter top), looking out, pleasant;
2. the same, his head tilted, his right hand tapping with its fingers at belt height;
3. head down, writing at belt height with a pencil in his right hand (the pencil from behind his right ear: so behind the right ear there is no pencil in this frame only, and the pencil is in his hand; the left-ear pencil stays);
4. leaning forward a little on both hands at belt height, smiling at the customer.

The same square pixel size, the same soft, flat shading and low texture as the barman -- NOT sharper, NOT more detailed, NOT brighter. Flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, nothing touching. No counter, no floor, no shadows, no text, no numbers, no border.
