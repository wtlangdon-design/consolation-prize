Image 1 shows two figures from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is Nickel Pratt, cut from image 2 as he stands on his claim with his rake. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his face is a handful of blocks; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: Pratt's claim in early morning. Pratt is the man with the rake. I need him drawn on his own in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE, as a sheet of twelve frames in three rows of four.

THE CHARACTER -- Nickel Pratt as image 2 shows him: a big, slow, broad-shouldered miner, battered brown hat, moustache, faded grey work shirt with rolled sleeves, dark trousers, brown boots. Unhurried, dogged, patient.

TOP ROW -- four poses with his rake, three-quarter view FACING LEFT, each showing him WHOLE, hat to boots:
1. REST: standing, both hands resting on top of the rake handle, the rake's head on the ground, as in image 2.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his chest very slightly fuller. Nothing else changes.
3. SKY: as pose 1, but his head tipped back, looking up at the sky, checking it.
4. RAKE: raking: leaning forward, both hands on the handle, the rake drawn across the ground in front of him.

MIDDLE ROW -- four frames of him WALKING to the RIGHT of the picture, seen from the side, unhurried, a wooden mallet in his right hand, no rake: a walk cycle (left foot forward; legs passing; right foot forward; legs passing).

BOTTOM ROW -- four poses with a wooden mallet, three-quarter view FACING LEFT, a short new wooden stake already standing in the ground in front of his boots in every frame:
1. MALLET REST: standing, the mallet hanging in his right hand.
2. MALLET BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his chest very slightly fuller.
3. MALLET UP: the mallet raised in both hands over the top of the stake.
4. MALLET DOWN: the mallet striking the top of the stake.

STYLE: every pose about 84 big pixels tall from hat to boots, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; the face as simple as Thad's -- a few blocks, dark blocks for the eyes.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No ground, no shadow, nothing else in the picture.
- Every frame is one piece -- the man and what he holds -- and does not touch any other frame.
- All frames in a row the same size, boots on the same baseline; clear magenta space between frames and between the rows.
- Between frames in a row, ONLY the change described happens. Same man, same colours, same clothes everywhere.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
