Image 1 is the town from a finished night map in my pixel-art adventure game: a top-down, slightly angled plan. A long row of two-storey wooden false-front buildings faces south onto a wide, open, muddy town square with a horse trough, and a road runs round the square. Behind the row is a narrow back lane with small shopfronts and a striped tent. The other images are references for the game's style only; do not copy anything from them into the map.
