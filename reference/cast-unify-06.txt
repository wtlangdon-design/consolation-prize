Image 1 is the hero of my pixel-art adventure game drawn large: THIS IS THE ART STYLE OF THE WHOLE GAME. Images 2 and 3 show townspeople already drawn correctly in that style: match their hand exactly -- the same size of square pixels, flat colour areas, soft simple shading with two or three tones per colour, very little texture, the same muted, slightly dusty palette, realistic proportions, the same dark outline treatment. Image 4 shows the people ALREADY in the game.

EVERY PERSON YOU DRAW MUST BE A DIFFERENT, RECOGNISABLE INDIVIDUAL: a different face, age, build, height, hairline, facial hair, posture and silhouette -- NOT the same man in different coloured clothes. Nobody may look like anyone in image 4.
