Image 1 is the layout for one room of my pixel-art adventure game: grey blocks for walls and furniture, and the hero (the man in the long coat) standing on the floor. Paint this room as a finished game background with two people in it, keeping the camera, the layout and every block where image 1 puts it. Paint the room ONLY inside the darker middle band of image 1, edge to edge; leave the light grey strips above and below it flat light grey, untouched. The whole room, from the ceiling to the floor in front of the hero, fits inside that band.

THE ROOM: the private office of the president of a land-and-mining company in a gold-rush town, 1850s, at night: Colonel Fanshawe's office. The richest room in town -- warm, generous, comfortable, expensive, and completely airless. Deep red damask walls, dark leather, gilt everywhere. Image 5 is a saloon on the same street at the same hour, for the world and the night light only.
- Far left: a heavy panelled door. Beside it a tall window over the night street, hung with velvet curtains the colour of good port.
- Left of middle: a big cork board on the wall covered with pinned survey maps, many small green pins and two red pins near the middle. Below it, a row of plush leather armchairs -- far too many chairs for the room -- all turned slightly toward the desk.
- Middle: a huge oil portrait in an ornate gilt frame of a young colonel in a gold-braided uniform, with a battle in the background. Beside it, a cavalry sabre in its scabbard mounted on the wall.
- In front of the portrait: a large polished desk with a green leather top, an oil lamp, a closed green leather-bound book at the right end, and a folded white silk handkerchief at the left end.
- Right of middle: a tall bookshelf of matching green-and-gilt ledgers. Below it, a long sideboard with wide shallow drawers, one not quite closed; on the sideboard a cut-glass decanter full of whisky.
- Far right: an enormous iron safe, the size of a small cabin, painted with roses and a little landscape with a golden river.
- Floor: a rich Turkey carpet.
- Light: the oil lamp on the desk, warm and close; the corners dimmer. No daylight.

THE PEOPLE (this picture is a reference painting, so people are allowed in it):
- The hero from image 4, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size, facing right toward the desk.
- COLONEL FANSHAWE, standing behind his desk, seen from the waist up over it, beaming at the hero, one hand open in welcome: a portly, prosperous gentleman of about sixty, silver hair, a fine silver moustache and side whiskers, ruddy cheeks, a black frock coat, a rich brocade waistcoat with a gold watch chain, a white shirt and a black silk cravat. The size of a standing man at that depth.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as images 2, 3 and 6, and the same hand as the hero: flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes, a visible pixel grid. Not sharp, not finely detailed, no hard internal contrast. Draw Fanshawe exactly as softly as the hero. This room has its own colours: deep red, dark leather brown, gilt gold, green leather, warm lamplight -- the most saturated room in the game. Nothing near black where the hero stands.

RULES: no text, letters or numbers anywhere -- the maps, the ledgers and the book carry marks, never words. No border, no frame around the picture, no caption.
