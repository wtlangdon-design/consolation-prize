- Row 1, ONE wide picture: the three judges C sitting side by side behind ONE long plain wooden table, seen from the front -- left to right the bald judge, the gaunt judge, the bearded judge -- each on a plain wooden chair, hands folded on the table top. Draw the WHOLE group: the table with its four legs, the chairs, and under the table each man's OWN legs and boots, matching his own clothes (the bald judge's brown trousers, the gaunt judge's black, the bearded judge's dark grey). The same scale as the hero (as tall as him, seated).

- Row 3, B, FULL FIGURE, CROUCHED low at the edge of water (do not draw the water), holding a round shallow gold pan in both hands in every pose, FIVE times: 1. crouched, the pan level in front of him; 2. peering into the pan; 3. tipping the pan out; 4. swirling the pan, tilted to the left; 5. swirling the pan, tilted to the right.

RULES: only the table, the chairs, the pan and their clothes. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta, no glow, no blur, no shadows, no ground, no text, no numbers, no border.
