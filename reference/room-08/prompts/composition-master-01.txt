THE ROOM: the sealed records archive behind a frontier claims office, 1850s, at night. Every claim and quarrel in the district is kept here in brown paper. Nobody is allowed in. Image 5 is another office in the same town at the same hour, for the world and the night light only.
- Left wall: a heavy plain door back to the office. Beside it, an oil lamp in an iron wall bracket, lit, with a neat scorched oval on the wall above it: the ONLY light in the room. A pigeonhole case of old letters on the wall beyond it.
- Back wall: tall shelves to the ceiling packed with boxes and bundles of brown paper, dusty, fading into darkness away from the lamp. A rolling library ladder on a rail leaning against them. On one shelf at chest height the dust is wiped away where someone has reached for years; on it, one box with a small brass corner piece, and bundles of papers tied with string.
- Left of centre, near the lamp: a small plain clerk's desk squared neatly, with a blotter, a pen, a stamp pad, a heavy brass seal with its handle wrapped in cloth, and a ledger; one drawer with a small brass label.
- Centre: a single sheet of paper under glass in a plain glass-topped display case on a stand. Beside it, one plain chair with a flat worn cushion, facing the shelves.
- Right wall: one small window high up, with iron bars set on the INSIDE of the frame; through it, the night street.
- Back right corner: a water stain spreading down the wall and ceiling, ruined brown paper, a bucket under the drip and a second bucket under the first.
- Floor: old dusty boards.
- The light: the lamp by the door lights the desk, the floor near the door and the front of the room; the deep shelves stay dark. No daylight.

THE PERSON: the hero from image 4, standing where image 1 puts him, at exactly that size. He is only there to show scale.

THE LOOK: the same pixel-art style as images 2, 3, 5 and 6, and the same hand as the hero: flat, soft shading, very little texture, simple shapes, a visible pixel grid. Not sharp, not finely detailed, no hard internal contrast. Paper, dust and brown; dark but not black where the hero stands.

RULES: no text, letters or numbers anywhere -- labels, boxes and papers carry marks, never words. No border, no frame, no caption.
