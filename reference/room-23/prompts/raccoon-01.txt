Image 1 shows two figures from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is the raccoon, cut from image 2 as he sits in the mouth of the hole. Look at Thad's pixels: BIG square blocks. Every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is a frame from the game: the claim at dusk. The fat raccoon sitting in the mouth of the hole in the hillside is the one I need, drawn on his own in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE, as a sheet of eight frames in two rows.

THE CHARACTER -- the raccoon, as image 2 shows him: fat, grey, a black mask, a ringed tail, small black hands; an expression of settled contempt. He owns the hole and he knows it.

TOP ROW -- four poses, sitting upright, FACING THE VIEWER, each showing him WHOLE, ears to tail:
1. REST: sitting, facing the viewer, hands on his belly, looking straight out with contempt.
2. BREATH: identical to pose 1 except his belly very slightly fuller. Nothing else changes.
3. LOOK: as pose 1, but his whole head turned to look to the LEFT of the picture, slow and disapproving.
4. EAT: still sitting, holding something small in both hands up to his mouth, chewing.

BOTTOM ROW -- four frames of him WALKING to the RIGHT of the picture on all fours, seen from the side, unhurried, tail out behind: a walk cycle (front left and back right forward; legs passing; front right and back left forward; legs passing).

STYLE: about 30 big pixels tall sitting, exactly as coarse as Thad; every pixel a large, crisp square block the size of Thad's; simple shapes and flat colour areas; his mask and the rings of his tail a few dark blocks.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No hole, no ground, no shadow, nothing else in the picture.
- All frames in a row the same size, feet on the same baseline; clear magenta space between frames and between the rows.
- Between frames, ONLY the change described happens. Same colours, same markings everywhere else.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- No text, labels, numbers or borders.
