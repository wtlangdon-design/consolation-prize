- Middle: in the hillside, a dark round hole into the rock, about the height of a crouching man, with old smooth edges -- and sitting in its mouth, a fat raccoon, looking out with great contempt.
- Right of the hole, further up: an older, darker boundary stake, sunk deeper.
- Above: a bare ridge against the sky.
- Lower right: a small low brown creek running past the foot of the claim.
- Light: dusk, cool and dim; the hole is the darkest thing on the hill.
