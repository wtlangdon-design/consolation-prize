Every row at the same scale: an ordinary man is the same height in all three rows (the height of the hero in the top row); women and slight men a little shorter, nobody shrunk. Every figure drawn with exactly the same size of square pixel as the three men in Image 2, the same amount of detail (no finer, no coarser), the same soft shading with two or three tones per colour and the same dark outline.

THE SHEET: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background everywhere around the figures. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta, no glow, no blur, no ground, no shadows, no text, no numbers, no border. Three rows, each row's feet on one line, clear space between figures.
