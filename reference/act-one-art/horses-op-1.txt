Draw the horses in THAT hand: chunky square pixels, big simple shapes, two or three flat tones per material (coat, mane, saddle, blanket, rail), soft broad shading, no shiny highlights, no fine hair strands, no fine detail on the tack, no hard dark lines inside the shapes.
