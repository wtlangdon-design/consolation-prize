Redraw these three townspeople AS IF THE SAME ARTIST WHO DREW THE HERO DREW THEM, at the same time, for the same game. Match the hero exactly in: the size of the square pixels, flat colour areas, soft simple shading with only two or three tones per colour, very little texture or fine detail, the same muted, slightly dusty palette, the same proportions (head size, limb thickness, hands, feet) and the same dark outline treatment. When in doubt, use LESS detail and SOFTER contrast. Their clothes keep their colours but lose saturation, to sit beside the hero's coat.

Three rows, one person per row, each person shown FOUR times side by side, full figure, facing the viewer, feet on the same line in each row, each figure about 280 pixels tall, every figure the same height as the others in its row and separated by clear magenta space. RULES: crisp pixel edges against the magenta, no glow, no blur, no shadow on the ground, no text, no numbers, no border.
