Image 1 shows characters from my pixel-art adventure game side by side at the same scale. On the left is Thad, the hero: he is the style standard. On the right is the man leaving tomorrow as he is drawn now -- and he is WRONG: the pixels are far too small and the detail far too fine. Look at Thad: his pixels are BIG square blocks, several times larger. Thad is only about 80 pixels tall from hair to shoes; every pixel of him is a clear, chunky square; his face is a handful of blocks -- one dark block for an eye, a few for the nose and mouth; his clothes are a few flat tones with no fine texture.

Image 2 is the man leaving tomorrow's sheet of five poses, drawn too finely. Redraw that same sheet -- the same character, the same clothes and colours, the same five poses in the same order, left to right -- in THAD'S PIXEL SIZE:
- Each pose about 80 big pixels tall from hat to boots, exactly as coarse as Thad: every pixel a large, crisp square block the same size as Thad's.
- Simple shapes and flat colour areas, the way Thad is drawn. No fine texture, no tiny highlights, no thin lines -- a feature too small for one of Thad's blocks is left out.
- The face as simple as Thad's: a few blocks, dark blocks for the eyes, the features still readable.
- The same warm, low night light as Thad; no brighter than him.

The character: THE MAN LEAVING TOMORROW: a tired middle-aged man in a buttoned blue-grey travelling coat and a brown hat, with a battered brown carpet bag.

The poses, as in image 2:
1. REST: standing, facing three-quarters to the viewer's left, hands in his coat pockets, the bag on the ground beside his boots on the viewer's left.
2. REACH: bending to take hold of the bag's handle.
3. LIFT: standing with the bag lifted a few inches off the ground, as if about to go.
4. SET DOWN: the bag back on the ground, one hand still on the handle, looking off down the road.
5. TALK: facing three-quarters left, hands in pockets, mouth open mid-word, the bag at his feet.

RULES -- these matter more than anything else:
- Background: flat, pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no shadow, nothing else in the picture.
- All five poses the same size, with the boots on the same baseline, each about two-thirds the height of the picture.
- Clear magenta space between poses. No pose touches or overlaps another.
- Between poses, ONLY the change described happens. Same clothes, same colours, same face everywhere else.
- Crisp pixel edges against the magenta. No soft glow or blur.
- Do not draw Thad. No text, labels, numbers or borders.
