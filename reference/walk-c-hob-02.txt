Image 2 is a WALK CYCLE that reads well in this game: six frames of another man walking RIGHT, left to right in order. Copy each frame's POSE exactly -- where each leg and foot is, how far the stride opens, the slight rise and fall of the body -- but draw the WATCHMAN of Image 1, not the man in Image 2. THE LEGS MUST TRADE PLACES, AND IT MUST SHOW: his NEAR leg (nearer the viewer) is drawn in lighter tones, his FAR leg darker, in shadow behind it. In frames 1-3 the NEAR (lighter) leg is the one stepping FORWARD; in frames 4-6 the FAR (darker) leg steps forward and the near leg trails behind -- exactly as in Image 2, where the lighter leg leads in the first three frames and the darker leg in the last three. Never the same leg in front in all six.

THE SHEET: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. ONE row of SIX full figures of the watchman walking RIGHT in profile, in Image 2's order, every figure the SAME height and scale, as large as fits, feet on one line, separated by clear magenta space. His lit lantern is held by its handle in his near hand in EVERY frame, swinging a little with the stride; his free arm swings opposite to his legs; the long coat moves with the stride and his legs show below its hem.

RULES: no ground, no shadows, no text, no numbers, no border. Crisp pixel edges against the magenta.
