## THE NUGGET'S DOORS → Room 3

**LOOK**
1. "The Bountiful Nugget. Batwing doors, which swing both ways, and which I suspect is the whole philosophy of the establishment."
2. "Somebody spent money on those doors and on nothing else. I have looked."
3. "The Nugget. It is bountiful in the way that a promise is bountiful."

**LISTEN**
1. "Talk, glasses, and a piano that is flat in one place. I could put it right in four minutes and nobody has asked me."
2. "Still flat. C-sharp. It is going to be C-sharp for the rest of my life."
3. "Somebody in there has told the same story four times and it has grown twice."

**Overrides**
> CLOSE — "They swing. Closing them is a temporary opinion."
> TALK TO — "I would have to go in. Going in costs money."
> PULL — "They swing outward, inward, and for some time afterwards."

## THE CLARION OFFICE → Room 9

**LOOK**
1. "The Consolation Clarion. One press, one man, and the front page of every issue for twelve years has been about a hole nobody can find."
2. "The door is open. The door is always open. He is desperate for somebody to say something quotable."
3. "There is a bill in the window advertising the Clarion, printed by the Clarion, on the Clarion's press."

**LISTEN**
1. "The press. It knocks on the return stroke, and it has knocked for years."
2. "Knocking. Still knocking."
3. "He is talking to himself in there. He is doing both sides of an interview."

**Overrides**
> TALK TO — "If I say anything within forty feet of that door it will be in print by four o'clock."
> CLOSE — "He would only open it again, and then write about it."

## THE IMPROVEMENT COMPANY → Room 10

**LOOK**
1. "CONSOLATION IMPROVEMENT COMPANY. The door is a great deal heavier than the building requires."
2. "Everyone who goes in comes out pleased. I have watched eleven men do it."
3. "Three storeys from here. I have been round the back and it is one."

**LISTEN**
1. "Nothing. It is the quietest doorway on the street and it is never still."
2. "Nothing. Somebody is always going in."
3. "Nothing at all. Not one raised voice in eleven weeks."

**Overrides**
> PUSH — "It opens inward, which is a decision somebody made."
> TALK TO — "I have spoken to the man behind that door. I came away with a map and I am no longer certain what I gave him."
> CLOSE — "It closes on its own, slowly, and without a sound. Somebody paid for that too."

## THE ROAD TO THE CLAIMS → Room 22

**LOOK**
1. "The road out to the diggings. Worn a foot deep by boots — which is more dirt than anybody out there has shifted on purpose."
2. "Two miles. It looks further in the morning and considerably further at night."
3. "Somewhere down there is forty feet of mud that is legally mine."

**LISTEN**
1. "Boots. A great many of them, going out. It is the most cheerful sound in Consolation and it happens at six in the morning."
2. "Nothing at the moment. They are all out there."
3. "Nothing coming back yet. They come back quiet."

**Overrides**
> PICK UP — "It is a road."
> PULL — "It is a road."
> TALK TO — "There is nobody on it."
> CLOSE — "I have considered it. Several of us have."

## THE ASSAY OFFICE → Room 5

*Added after Room 5 was composed and written. It was the one Room 2 exit doc 14 never covered.*

**LOOK**
1. "The assay office. A door, a step, and one window. It is the only front on this street that is not selling itself."
2. "No sign. Everybody knows where it is and nobody needs telling twice."
3. "The step is worn pale in the middle and nowhere else. They come, they stand, they go."

**LISTEN**
1. "Nothing. It is the quietest working building in Consolation."
2. "Nothing. A pen, possibly. I may be inventing the pen."
3. "Nothing at all. Whatever is decided in there is decided without discussion."

**Overrides**
> PUSH — "It opens inward, and it opens easily. Somebody has oiled it."
> CLOSE — "It closes itself, slowly, and latches. Somebody sees to that."
> TALK TO — "I have tried talking to it. The building is not the problem."

## Notes

1. **"Nothing coming back yet. They come back quiet."** pairs with Room 22's own hotspots — men going out talk over each other, men coming back say nothing. Heard from Main Street before the player has ever walked out there.
2. **The Improvement Company's TALK TO** is the Fanshawe rule stated by a man who does not yet know it is a rule: he came away with a map and is no longer sure what he gave for it.
3. **The assay office's overrides are Winnie's character in two lines.** The door is oiled and it latches itself. Nobody maintains a door in Consolation. She maintains hers, and the game never says so.
4. **"C-sharp for the rest of my life"** is the same joke as the boardwalk's B flat. Two of the nine hotspots and one exit now have Thad involuntarily pitching the town. That is the seeding rate the LISTEN endgame needs.
