## THE NUGGET'S DOORS → Room 3

**LOOK**
1. "The Bountiful Nugget. Batwing doors, which swing both ways. I suspect that's the whole philosophy of the place."
2. "Somebody spent money on those doors and nothing else. I've looked."
3. "The Nugget. It's bountiful the way a promise is bountiful."

**LISTEN**
1. "Talk, glasses, and a piano that's flat in one place. I could put it right in four minutes, and nobody's asked me."
2. "Still flat. C-sharp. It's going to be C-sharp for the rest of my life."
3. "Somebody in there is telling a story about a bear. The bear is bigger than it was."

**Overrides**
> CLOSE — "They swing. Closing them is a temporary opinion."
> TALK TO — "I'd have to go in. Going in costs money."
> PULL — "They swing outward, inward, and for some time afterwards."

## THE CLARION OFFICE → Room 9

**LOOK**
1. "The Consolation Clarion. One press, one man, and the front page of every issue for twelve years has been about a hole nobody can find."
2. "The door's open. The door's always open. He's desperate for somebody to say something quotable."
3. "There's a bill in the window advertising the Clarion, printed by the Clarion, on the Clarion's press."

**LISTEN**
1. "The press. It knocks on the return stroke. It's knocked for years."
2. "Knocking. Still knocking."
3. "He's talking to himself in there. Both sides of an interview. He's winning."

**Overrides**
> TALK TO — "If I say anything within forty feet of that door, it'll be in print by four o'clock. Spelled wrong."
> CLOSE — "He'd only open it again, and then write about it."

## THE IMPROVEMENT COMPANY → Room 10

**LOOK**
1. "CONSOLATION IMPROVEMENT COMPANY. The door is a great deal heavier than the building needs."
2. "Everyone who goes in comes out pleased. I've watched eleven men do it. I'd like to be the twelfth."
3. "Three stories from here. I've been round the back. It's one."

**LISTEN**
1. "Not a sound. The quietest doorway on the street, and it's never still."
2. "Not a sound, and somebody is always going in."
3. "Nothing at all. Not one raised voice in eleven weeks."

**Overrides**
> PUSH — "It opens inward, which is a decision somebody made."
> TALK TO — "I've spoken to the man behind that door. I came away with a map, and I'm no longer sure what I gave him."
> CLOSE — "It closes on its own, slowly, and without a sound. Somebody paid for that too."

## THE ROAD TO THE CLAIMS → Room 22

**LOOK**
1. "The road out to the diggings. Worn a foot deep by boots — which is more dirt than anybody out there has shifted on purpose."
2. "Two miles. It looks further in the morning and considerably further at night."
3. "Somewhere down there is forty feet of mud that's legally mine. I can't wait to see it."

**LISTEN**
1. "Boots. A great many of them, going out. The most cheerful sound in Consolation, and it happens at six in the morning."
2. "Nothing at the moment. They're all out there."
3. "Nothing coming back yet. They come back quiet."

**Overrides**
> PICK UP — "It's a road. It's two miles long. No."
> PULL — "It's a road. It's been pulled about enough."
> TALK TO — "There's nobody on it."
> CLOSE — "I've considered it. Several of us have."

## THE ASSAY OFFICE → Room 5

*Added after Room 5 was composed and written. It was the one Room 2 exit doc 14 never covered.*

**LOOK**
1. "The assay office. A door, a step, and one window. The only front on this street that isn't selling itself."
2. "No sign. Everybody knows where it is and nobody needs telling twice."
3. "The step is worn pale in the middle and nowhere else. They come, they stand, they go."

**LISTEN**
1. "Hardly a sound. Somebody in there is working, and doing it quietly."
2. "Nothing. A pen, possibly. I may be inventing the pen."
3. "Whatever's decided in there is decided without discussion."

**Overrides**
> PUSH — "It opens inward, and easily. Somebody oils it."
> CLOSE — "It closes itself, slowly, and latches. Somebody sees to that."
> TALK TO — "I've tried talking to it. The building isn't the problem."

## Notes

1. **"Nothing coming back yet. They come back quiet."** pairs with Room 22's own hotspots — men going out talk over each other, men coming back say nothing. Heard from Main Street before the player has ever walked out there.
2. **The Improvement Company's TALK TO** is the Fanshawe rule stated by a man who does not yet know it is a rule: he came away with a map and is no longer sure what he gave for it.
3. **The assay office's overrides are Winnie's character in two lines.** The door is oiled and it latches itself. Nobody maintains a door in Consolation. She maintains hers, and the game never says so.
4. **"C-sharp for the rest of my life"** is the same joke as the boardwalk's B flat. Two of the nine hotspots and one exit now have Thad involuntarily pitching the town. That is the seeding rate the LISTEN endgame needs.
