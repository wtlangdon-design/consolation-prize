# CONSOLATION PRIZE
## The Examine Layer — LOOK AT / LISTEN TO

*Companion to Bible v2, the Puzzle Graph, the Liar's Assay, and the Dialogue Trees. 35 rooms · ~520 hotspots · ~1,040 lines.*

---

# PART ONE — DOCTRINE

## The split

This is the single most important craft decision in the game, and everything else follows from it.

> **LOOK AT is the comedy. LISTEN TO is the truth.**

**LOOK AT** gives you the object and Thad's flat assessment of it. It is where the jokes live. It is Thad performing his relentless, procedural optimism at a world that does not deserve it.

**LISTEN TO** gives you what the object is *doing*, or what it is failing to do. It is stranger, more oblique, shorter, and frequently sad. **In a town where every institution runs on perjury, listening is the only thing that never lies.** The game's entire melancholy lives in this verb, and the player will absorb that long before they can articulate it.

This is why the protagonist is a piano tuner, why the score is flat, and why the last puzzle is silent.

## Rules

1. **LISTEN never lies.** Not once, in ~520 lines. This is the contract that makes Act IV work.
2. **LISTEN is shorter than LOOK.** Usually much shorter. Often a fragment. Sometimes one word.
3. **Never explain the joke across the pair.** LISTEN is not the punchline to LOOK. They are two different registers looking at the same object.
4. **Silence is a valid response and should be used often** — but the *phrasing* of nothing must vary. "Nothing." / "Nothing at all." / "Nothing, and it's trying." / "Not a thing. Not even the wind bothers."
5. **The three load-bearing LISTEN lines must be indistinguishable in tone from the five hundred that aren't.** No emphasis, no music sting, no highlighting. If the player can tell which lines matter, the entire design collapses.

## The three that matter

| Room | Hotspot | Line | Pays off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prosperity — the hole | The hole | *"Air. Moving. It's warmer than the day."* | F1, twenty hours later |
| Registrar's archive | The timesheet | *"Paper. Same hand as the filing."* | E9 |
| The listening chamber | Everything | *(the puzzle)* | F2 |

Note the first one is available in **Act I, puzzle five**. The player will read it, use it to evict a raccoon, and forget it completely.

## ROOM 1 — STAGE ROAD & COACH STOP
*Night. Opening screen. Six hotspots.*

**THE TOWN SIGN** — *CONSOLATION · POP. 2,000 AND CLIMBING*
> **LOOK:** "The number has been repainted four times. You can see the smaller ones underneath, each of them once true."
> **LISTEN:** "It creaks. It would like to be taken down."

**THE MUD**
> **LOOK:** "Mud. I have been assured it improves."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Mud is the only honest thing in the territory and it has nothing to say."

**THE COACH** *(before it departs)*
> **LOOK:** "It is leaving. It has the confident manner of a thing that will not be coming back."
> **LISTEN:** "The near axle is dry. It will be heard from."

**HIS CASE**
> **LOOK:** "Everything I own, and a tuning fork. The tuning fork is the valuable part."
> **LISTEN:** "The fork, faintly, against the clasp. A. Four hundred and forty. It is the only correct thing within a hundred miles."

**THE WATCHMAN'S LAMP** *(receding)*
> **LOOK:** "A lamp, going away. It has not once looked back at me."
> **LISTEN:** "Footsteps. Even ones. He is counting them."

**THE ROAD EAST**
> **LOOK:** "Home is that way. It is eleven hundred miles that way, which robs the gesture of some of its force."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing coming."

## ROOM 32 — INSIDE THE COFFIN *(E7)*
***Special case. Zero hotspots. No verb panel.***

The panel does not fade — it is simply gone, between one frame and the next. There is no LOOK. **There is no LISTEN.**

This is the point, and it must be stated plainly in the technical spec so it is not implemented as a bug: **the game takes the LISTEN verb away from a man whose entire identity is listening, and gives him nothing to hear but dirt.** No music. No narration. No asides. Three minutes.

When Hob digs him out, **the first thing that returns is the verb panel**, before the art has finished resolving. The player will feel it come back.

---

## ROOM 33 — THE LISTENING CHAMBER *(F2)*
***Special case. LOOK is deliberately useless. LISTEN is the interface.***

**EVERY ROCK SURFACE — LOOK:**
> "Rock."
> "Rock. Identical to the last rock."
> "This is the same rock. I am confident it is the same rock."
> "Rock. I have stopped drawing distinctions."

*The LOOK verb — the game's comedy engine for twenty hours — is systematically emptied out over about thirty selections. It is the funniest running joke in the game and it exists to teach the player, without a single line of instruction, to stop looking.*

**LISTEN** returns pitch, not prose. The player is hearing resonance and hunting for the hollow. The score is coming into tune underneath, one interval at a time, over ninety seconds.

The only prose line in the entire room, on the correct spot:

> **LISTEN:** "…There's a room down there."

# PART FOUR — VOLUME & HONESTY

| | Lines |

| Scripted above (12 rooms, fully or substantially) | ~190 |
| Manifest rooms (23 rooms, ~210 hotspots × 2) | ~420 |
| Room 36 rewrite | ~18 |
| Act-state variants (rooms that change between acts) | ~140 |
| Barks and repeat-selection variants | ~120 |
| **Examine total** | **~890** |

Plus ~2,250 dialogue lines and the Assay. **Roughly 3,150 written lines for the finished game.**

**A straight assessment of what's left:** the twelve rooms above contain every load-bearing line, every special case, and enough range to fix the voice. What remains is about 420 lines of genuine volume work — not design, not craft decisions, just writing to a fixed calibration. That's the one part of this project that benefits from being done in passes rather than in one sitting, and it's the part where quality degrades fastest if it's rushed or generated. I'd do it a room at a time.

## Implementation warnings

1. **No emphasis on the three load-bearing lines.** No sting, no highlight, no different colour, no achievement. If the player can tell, the design is dead.
2. **The coffin's missing verb panel is not a bug.** Document it in the spec or it will be "fixed."
3. **The listening chamber's degrading LOOK lines are not filler.** They are a thirty-line joke that teaches a mechanic.
4. **Repeat-selection variants matter most in Room 2**, which the player will visit more than any other screen. Three variants minimum per hotspot.
