# PART ONE — GLOBAL VERB FALLBACKS

Used when no object-specific line exists. Rotate within a pool; never repeat consecutively.

**PICK UP**
> "I gave it a good try. It stays where it is."
> "I'd better not. It's part of the town, and I'm hoping the town will like me."

*Errata 168 (Tyler, 2026-11-07, on picking up the trough's water: "it says it has opinions??? That doesn't even make sense"): "I have tried. It has views." and "That is not mine, and more to the point it will not come." are retired. This pool answers 347 things, among them water, mud, the sky and whole buildings, and those two lines fit only a solid object that could resist. The two that stay are true of anything.*

**USE**
> "I've no plan for it yet. Give me time; I'm full of plans."
> "Not usefully. Not yet."
> "I'd need to want something from it first, and for once I don't."
> "That would want a purpose, and I'm saving mine for the big things."

**OPEN**
> "It doesn't open. Very little here does, but I live in hope."
> "There's no way into it. I checked, thoroughly and with optimism."
> "It isn't that kind of thing, more's the pity."

**CLOSE**
> "It's already as closed as it means to be."
> "It isn't open. I'd have noticed. I notice things."
> "There's nothing to close. I'm almost disappointed."

**PUSH**
> "It doesn't move. I've committed to this now, and it still doesn't move."
> "Nothing. And I put my back into it, too."
> "It stays. I'll give it this: it knows its own mind."

**PULL**
> "It stays where it is, and I look like a man pulling at a thing."
> "No. Not for want of trying."
> "It isn't coming. I can respect a thing that knows where it belongs."

**TALK TO** *(non-persons)*
> "I've decided against it. I'll save my charm for something that can answer."
> "It's said nothing, I've said nothing, and we're both perfectly content."
> "I'd like it on record that I considered it."

# PART TWO — OBJECT-SPECIFIC OVERRIDES

Where a specific line exists it takes precedence over the pool.

**THE FALSE FRONTS**
> PUSH — "I give the front of a building a friendly push. It doesn't fall over. I'm honestly a little surprised, and rather proud of it."
> PULL — "If I pull hard enough, the whole town comes down. I haven't decided how I feel about that."

**THE IMPROVEMENT COMPANY SIGN**
> PULL — "It's screwed on, thoroughly, by somebody who was paid extra to be thorough. I respect a man who earns his money."
> TALK TO — "Talking to a sign is beneath a man of business. I'll talk to the man inside, the moment he'll see me."

**THE WATER TROUGH**
> USE — "I drank from it once, in a spirit of adventure. The “once” is doing a great deal of work in that sentence." *(doc 49 supersedes the original line here)*
> OPEN — "It's a trough. It's as open as a thing can be, and I admire that in a trough."

**THE BOARDWALK**
> PULL — "The loose board declines to come up. It is loose recreationally, not professionally." *(doc 49 supersedes)*
> PUSH — "Third from the end. Up an inch, down an inch! I did that four more times than I needed to, and enjoyed every one."

**POSTED NOTICES**
> PICK UP — "I wouldn't dream of it. They belong to the town. Though I've read them four times now, which makes me practically a shareholder."
> OPEN — "They're facing out already, bless them. Showing everything to everybody is a notice's whole career."

**THE CHURCH STEEPLE**
> PICK UP — "It's four hundred yards away and forty feet tall. I have big plans, but not that big."
> TALK TO — "I'm not going to shout at a church from the street. I was raised better than that. Slightly."

**A DOG**
> TALK TO — "How do you do? Thaddeus Grubb, tuner of pianos, soon to be a man of considerable means. He's taken the news very calmly." *(The dog does not respond. It is the most dignified exchange I have had in Consolation.)*
> PICK UP — "I couldn't possibly. We haven't been properly introduced."
> PUSH — "Push him? Look at that face. I'd sooner push the church."

**THE MUD**
> PICK UP — "I pick up some mud. I am now a man holding mud, in a street, at night. I put it back."
> USE — "Use it on what? I'm open to suggestions, but not to mud."

**THE HILLS**
> PICK UP — "They're hills. I'm ambitious, but I'm not unreasonable."
> TALK TO — "No."
> USE — "I wouldn't know where to begin, and neither would they."
