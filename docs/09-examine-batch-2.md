## ROOM 4 — THE NUGGET BACK ROOM
*Where the Liar's Assay is held. Eight hotspots.*

**THE JUDGES' TABLE**
> **LOOK:** "A table, three chairs, and a great deal of authority for a room behind a saloon."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is waiting to be sat at, which is most of what furniture does here."

**THE PENCIL**
> **LOOK:** "A pencil, on a string, nailed to the table. Every dispute in this district for eleven years has been settled with this pencil."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE TALLY BOARD** — ***the plant***
> **LOOK:** "Duels won, by name, in chalk, going back years. Deke Vessel has forty-one. Nickel Pratt has nine, which surprises me. Colonel Fanshawe has none — because Colonel Fanshawe has never entered."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

*Fanshawe has never taken part in the institution he presides over. Duel Four is the first of his life, which is why he mistakes it for a formality.*

**THE SECOND RULEBOOK**
> **LOOK:** "A longer copy of the rules, in a binder, with amendments. There are fourteen amendments. Eleven of them concern seating."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE RING OF CHAIRS**
> **LOOK:** "Chairs, in a ring, close in. Whatever happens in here happens with everyone's knees nearly touching."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing yet."

**THE CHALK** · **THE LAMP** · **THE DOOR TO THE BAR**
> *(The door — **LISTEN:** "The bar. Muffled. Somebody out there has told the same story four times and it has grown twice.")*

## ROOM 10 — IMPROVEMENT COMPANY LOBBY
*Ten hotspots. Every one of them is generous.*

**THE MODEL**
> **LOOK:** "A scale model of Consolation as it will be. A bank, an opera house, a second church, a park. It is beautifully made. It has been beautifully made for nine years."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE CHAIRS**
> **LOOK:** "Six chairs, all of them better than any chair I have sat in since Providence. A man could wait here a long time and not mind."
> **LISTEN:** "Leather, settling. That is the sound of somebody deciding to stay a bit longer."

**THE MAP WALL**
> **LOOK:** "Maps to Mott's strike, framed, for sale. Eleven different maps, all of them authoritative, all of them different."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE TESTIMONIALS**
> **LOOK:** "Framed letters of thanks from satisfied claimholders. Four of them. I have read the dates. They are all from the first two years."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE SUBSCRIPTION BOOK**
> **LOOK:** "Shares in the Company, at a dollar. A long list of names, and beside each a small number, and the numbers were larger nearer the top of the page."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE STOVE**
> **LOOK:** "A stove, lit, in a lobby, in July. Nobody who comes in here is ever cold."
> **LISTEN:** "Burning. Paper, I think, rather than wood."

**THE CLERK'S DESK** · **THE RUG** · **THE WINDOW** · **THE INNER DOOR**
> *(The rug — **LOOK:** "Turkish, or claiming to be. The only floor covering in Consolation, and it goes right up to the mud at the threshold.")*

## ROOM 13 — THE UNDERTAKER'S
*Nine hotspots. Nothing here is wasted.*

**THE COFFINS**
> **LOOK:** "Coffins, stacked by size. There are a great many in the smallest size, which I have decided to attribute to storage efficiency."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Naturally nothing. I don't know what I expected."

**THE JAR OF TEETH**
> **LOOK:** "A jar of teeth. He sells them on to the barber, who fits them to the living. Nothing in this establishment is wasted."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Obviously nothing. I checked, and I regret it."

**THE GOOD SUIT**
> **LOOK:** "One good suit, in three sizes, rented for viewings and returned afterwards. Half the men in Boot Hill were buried in their own clothes and viewed in this."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "Every burial in Consolation. Eleven pages. Two thousand people have lived here for twelve years and it is eleven pages, which means most of them left."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE TABLE**
> **LOOK:** "Scrubbed to the grain. It is the cleanest surface in the territory."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE PRICE LIST** · **THE TOOLS** · **THE WAITING BENCH** · **THE WINDOW**
> *(The price list — **LOOK:** "Plain, Respectable, and Handsome. The Handsome is four times the Plain and the difference is a handle.")*

**GRIEVANCE**
> **LOOK:** "A mule. He has looked at me once, at length, and has not felt the need to do it again."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. He is holding his breath. I am almost certain he is holding his breath."

**GRIEVANCE'S STALL**
> **LOOK:** "His name is burned into the post. The other stalls are numbered. Somebody, at some point, decided this animal warranted a name."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**GRIEVANCE'S BLANKET**
> **LOOK:** "A blanket, folded, considerably better than the blanket on my bed."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE OTHER MULES**
> **LOOK:** "Four mules, numbered, entirely willing to work. They are looking at Grievance the way a work crew looks at a man who has got away with something."
> **LISTEN:** "Contented. They have no idea."

**THE TACK**

**THE HAY** · **THE LIVERY MAN'S CHAIR** · **THE BOARD OF NAMES**
> *(The board — **LOOK:** "Who has hired what, going back years. Twelve years ago somebody hired Grievance for four days and did not write down where they were going." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*

## ROOM 16 — MADAME OZYMANDIA'S TENT

**THE PRICE LIST**

> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE SECOND ROD**

**THE CHART**

## ROOM 17 — CHURCH & MEETING HALL
*Ten hotspots. The tallest building in town and the only one as deep as it is tall.*

**THE PEWS**
> **LOOK:** "Pews built from the crates the piano came in. The third one still reads PROVIDENCE R I, which is where I am from. I have sat down."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE HYMNAL**
> **LOOK:** "One hymnal, on the lectern, for a congregation of two hundred."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Nobody opens it. They sing what they remember, and they remember it wrong, and they remember it wrong *together*, which is nearly as good."

**THE BELL ROPE**
> **LOOK:** "A rope, going up through a hole in the ceiling to the flattest bell in the territory."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing, at rest. It is the only time it is bearable."

**THE SUBSCRIPTION LIST**
> **LOOK:** "Who paid for what. The Colonel paid for the roof, the windows, the bell, and the stove, and his name is on all four in gilt."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE WINDOW**
> **LOOK:** "Real glass, freighted four hundred miles. The single most expensive object in Consolation, and it looks out at mud."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE COLLECTION PLATE**
> **LOOK:** "Empty, and polished. It has been polished a great deal more than it has been filled."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE PULPIT** · **THE STOVE** · **THE FLOOR** · **THE NOTICE OF SERVICES**
> *(The floor — **LOOK:** "Boards, laid properly, tongued and grooved. Somebody built this building to last and every other building on this street to sell.")*

## New plant — back-propagate to the Bible

**Room 4's tally board** establishes that Fanshawe has never entered the Liar's Assay in eleven years. Duel Four is the first of his life. That is why he treats it as a formality, and why the man who controls every institution in Consolation loses at the only one he has never actually played.
