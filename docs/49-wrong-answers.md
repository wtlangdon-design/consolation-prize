# 49 · THE WRONG-ANSWER LAYER — authored refusals, room by room

> **Status: R5 ruled (density as proposed, adopted by Tyler), pass complete.** The layer doc 47
> identified as the unbudgeted gap: doc 05 owns LOOK and LISTEN, doc 02 owns the ~45 correct
> uses, and everything else a player tries fell to `verbFallbacks`' generic refusals. Half of a
> Monkey Island room's comedy lives in the wrong answers. This document is that half.

## Doctrine

1. **Each room authors its funny diagonal, not its full grid.** Five to eight wrong-verb
   responses per town room, fewer for the late quiet rooms — the best jokes available, chosen
   across the seven non-examine verbs (PICK UP, TALK TO, OPEN, CLOSE, USE, PUSH, PULL).
   Everything unauthored still falls to `verbFallbacks`, which is correct: the fallbacks are
   the floor, this layer is the furniture.
2. **A wrong answer never punishes and never hints.** It is flavour with Thad's fingerprints
   on it. If a line points at a solution, it moves to doc 02's territory and out of here.
3. **TALK TO an object is the royal verb of this layer.** Thad talking to things — and the
   things declining to answer — is the loneliest and funniest thing the verb set can do, and
   it seeds Act IV's Listening: he has been addressing the inanimate all game; eventually
   something answers.
4. **LISTEN's honesty rule does not bind this layer, but Thad's character does.** He can be
   wrong, hopeful, or defeated; he cannot be cruel, and the town's objects cannot lie in ways
   that mislead a puzzle.
5. **Compiler format:** `**HOTSPOT** · VERB — "line"` under a `## ROOM` heading. Compiles to
   the hotspot's `responses[VERB]`, same as doc 05's pairs.
