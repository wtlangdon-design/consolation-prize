## What was missing

Doc 17 kept Mott out of the stage driver's mouth, rightly: "The town explains itself, contradicting itself as it goes." But nothing in the first rooms did explain it:
- The map seller's first menu line was "Is that a map to Mott's strike?", asked by a man who had been in the territory ten minutes and had never heard of Mott.
- Nobody said why two thousand people live in a town with no gold, or who does well out of it.
- Thad's two errands, the letter to Pike and a bed he cannot afford, were said once each on the stage road and never again. The hotel's piano is the first puzzle, and nothing sent him to the hotel.

## What Monkey Island does

The lookout gives Guybrush a destination. The ordinary pirates in the Scumm Bar tell him about the island and about LeChuck, each from his own angle. The three important-looking pirates turn his want ("I want to be a pirate") into errands. Every piece of exposition is a joke from somebody with his own reason for telling it, and it ends in something to do.

## Ours

**The want becomes errands, on Main Street, in Thad's own words.** His first sight of the town ends with what he means to do about it:

> "So this is Consolation."
> "Half of it is two stories tall in front and one story deep."
> "The other half is real, and is doing better."
> "Two errands, then. Mr. Pike, and a bed."
> "The hotel wants a dollar more than I have. I have a very good ear, which must be worth a dollar to somebody."

The ear is his trade and his tuning fork; the hotel is in front of him. It names the errand and the means, never the solution (invariant 4: no hint system).

**The town explains itself, in three voices on the same street**, each from its own angle (doc 99), each true of what the town believes and none of it the truth beneath (doc 01: the fraud, the Company's real interest and Mott's whereabouts stay where the reveal schedule puts them). Each can be met first, so each says its piece without leaning on the others.
- **The map seller, the true believer:** who Mott was, the strike, and the town that grew while it waited. In doc 07 under him: "Who's Mott?"
- **The pie woman, the seller:** where the money goes. The Company sells the searchers everything they stand on. In doc 07 under her: "Is there money to be made in Consolation?"
- **The man leaving tomorrow, the one who stayed:** why people came, and why they do not leave. In doc 07 under him: "What brought you here, in the first place?"

**What stays out.** Fanshawe is named by nobody here; the Company is. His inversion (he wants the search to go on) lands at the end of Act II, and nothing here says more than the town already says on its shopfronts: the Company's business is the search.
