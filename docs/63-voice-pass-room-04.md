# THE LAST CLAIM IN CONSOLATION
## 63 · The voice pass, Room 4 — before and after

*Errata 100. The back room of the Nugget, where the Liar's Assay is held. A lighter pass than Main Street: these lines were already closer to doc 59. The edits are contractions, fewer "Nothing." openings, Thad in the first person where a line said "you", and the bear story from the Nugget's corner heard through the door. Changed in docs 05, 09, 49 and 54 and re-extracted.*

*Unchanged: the duels and every boast and counter (doc 03), which are puzzle pairs; the scoreboard's names and numbers; the fourteen amendments and the seating; every flag and gate.*
