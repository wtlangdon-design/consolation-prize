# 106 · The ways out — LOOK and LISTEN for every silent door and exit

*Tyler, 2026-11-08. Told that forty doors and exits answered LOOK and LISTEN with a generic line, he chose "Write them all" (errata 189, the seventh writing exception). These are written in one voice, under doc 59 and doc 99.*

*The rules:*
- *Each way gets three LOOK lines and three LISTEN lines.*
- *Every line is true in every act and state in which the way can be clicked.*
- *Nothing describes what the plate does not show.*
- *Nothing marks a puzzle, and nothing hints.*
- *LISTEN reports a sound that is there, or says that there is none.*
- *A way is named here exactly as its hover label reads.*

*Not written: Room 33's THE GAP, which leads out of the listening chamber. That room's LISTEN is its interface, and no line may be added to it (invariant 3). Also not written: Boot Hill's PIKE'S NEIGHBOR, which is the plot's own hotspot and already speaks for it.*

*`tools/compile-room.mjs` reads a way that is an examined subject (`exitSubjects` with `linesFrom`). `tools/extract-content.mjs` (`waysOut`) writes every other way into its room file.*
