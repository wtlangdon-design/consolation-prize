## What it is

Fiddler's Gulch as half an act: a placer camp that struck real gold, reached through an old mine whose way is found with Ozymandia's survey, where Thad finds Vessel and loses Duel One in the Gulch's rough Assay. It replaces the ride over black that today takes Thad to the Nugget at B3 (errata 73 B, errata 88 E). Duel Two stays in the Nugget's back room.

The proposals and Tyler's six decisions that led here are in the Docs artifact "Leaving Consolation: two proposals" (2026-11-08): Option 2, half an act; a placer camp, the camp that struck it; the Gulch keeps a rougher Assay of its own; the mine, as the way into the Gulch; Sump's third copy; Thad hires the rod from Ozymandia.

## The chain

Fed from the side: A9's third copy and G1's rod into G2; B1's Clarion edition into G3; G8's favors, optional, into Duel Two.

## The light

**Day, throughout (Tyler, 2026-11-08).** The washout, the ledge and the five Gulch screens are drawn in daylight. The two days each way pass on the ride, over black, as they do in the game today, so nobody sleeps on screen. The mine has no daylight to read the time by. Time of day stays an authored visual state (errata 64d); nothing here is a clock.

Ten new screens: one where the road gives out, four in the mine, five in the Gulch. Nothing in the Gulch has a false front, a boardwalk or an office. It is canvas, logs and river, hemmed in by the ravine.

"Bar" is the miners' own word for a gravel bar in a river. The Gulch holds its Assay on the bar, which is the joke. The pun is in the place, not in a line.

## The people

Four new people, and Vessel. The camp found real gold, and it has made every one of them rich, suspicious and unhappy: the mirror of Consolation, which has found nothing and is cheerful about it.

| Person | Who they are | Their one joke |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vessel | Keeps the grog tent under another name, selling things to men who can finally afford them. | He has never been happier. He has found a town where everyone has money. |
| The rule-keeper | Keeps the Gulch's Assay. There is no handbill: he recites the rules from memory, and gets them wrong. | Sowerby's opposite: brief, confident and wrong. |
| Two panners | Rich men standing in cold water all day because they can't think what else to do. | Each is sure the other is hiding a bigger nugget. They are the Gulch's teachers. |
| The suspicious one | Meets every stranger at the foot of the ledge path, because every stranger is a claim jumper. | The only thing that will reassure him is proof that you have nothing. |

All are drawn against Thad in the Gulch's screens, under the character style gate. Vessel is redrawn for the Gulch's light and scale from his accepted figure.

**They don't know Thad's reputation.** The news from Consolation hasn't reached the Gulch, so their barks don't change with it: the one place nobody has heard of him.

## The mine

Four screens between the washout and the ledge, each holding one clause of the survey Thad carries. Short, forgiving and funny, or not built.

| Screen | The ways out | The right way, by the survey | A wrong way leads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The adit | Three openings | The one with the stake | Back out to the washout |
| The climb | Two passages | The one that rises | Back to the adit |
| The two rocks | Two passages | Past the larger rock | Back to the climb |
| The line | Two passages that look the same | The one where the rod goes still | Round in a loop to the line again |

- **A wrong turn costs one screen, never the whole mine.** A player who ignores the survey gets through in a few minutes of trying both ways: the forest's rule, and the reason this maze is allowed.
- **The line, with the rod.** USE THE BRASS ROD there and it quivers toward one passage and goes still toward the other: "until the humming stopped". The humming is shown, in the rod and in Thad, not heard. No sound in the mine tells the player which way to go.
- **The line, without the rod.** Both passages are tried by walking; the wrong one comes back to the line.
- **Once through, the way is known.** After the first crossing the washout and the ledge join directly, and the mine is walked as one passage.
- **Engine.** Exits that lead back to the same few rooms (existing); an item used on an exit (new); a direct way opened by a flag (ordinary).

## Rules it keeps

- **No dead ends.** Grievance waits at the washout until Thad comes back; the mine is walkable both ways; if he leaves the Gulch before G9, he can return.
- **Duel Two can still be won without G8.** The Gulch's favors are extra counters, never the only source.
- **He can't die; the game can't become unwinnable.** No falls, no air running out, no cave-ins. The automated traversal is extended to the trip.
- **The listening stays special.** Nothing on the trip is solved by LISTEN. G5 is USE, the fork on the fiddle; the mine is read with the eyes.
- **No hint system, and nothing marked.** The survey is a document in the world, not a hint.
- **The invariants are untouched.** HOB_C1 option 4, the three load-bearing LISTEN lines, Room 32, the detune and the letters home are outside the trip and not referenced from it.
- **Ozymandia isn't given away.** The rod works, and nobody remarks on it.
- **The style gate holds.** Every new figure is made against Thad in the room he stands in.

**B1 is always before the trip.** B3 already refuses until `T_VESSEL_LOCATION` is set, and only B1 sets it, so the edition is in hand whenever the trip can start (settled 2026-11-08, from the design's open questions).
