## 130 · No parentheticals: the audit, and the pass (errata 308)

*Tyler, 2026-11-14, told that Monkey Island printed nothing on screen but what people say: "I don't like the parentheticals. Id prefer to find other ways to make all the parentheticals happen (dialogue, actions, movements, etc)". Errata 308 records the ruling and the permission for the lines it needs, which are written in the documents the directions came from, in one voice, under doc 59 and doc 99.*

It reverses two earlier rulings: doc 58's (a direction is shown, in parentheses, in the quieter ink) and doc 95's confirmation (the directions whose wording carries the joke stay printed). When the pass is done, nothing in parentheses reaches the screen.

# PART ONE — THE WAYS

Every printed direction goes one of six ways. Monkey Island used all six.

| Way | What happens | Monkey Island |
|---|---|---|
| **CUT** | The direction goes, because what is said or shown already carries it | Most of what a stage direction would say is simply not there |
| **THAD** | Thad says it, in the first person, as he says every LOOK line | Guybrush describing what just happened: "That didn't work." |
| **THEM** | The other person says it, in their own voice | Characters narrating their own business as they do it |
| **ACT** | A figure performs it, in frames it already has; nothing printed (doc 95 class A) | The whole of the animation |
| **POSE** | It needs a pose not yet drawn. It goes on the image list (`reference/acted-directions-3/brief.md`), and until it is drawn the line around it carries it, or it is cut | — |
| **PAUSE** | A pause, worded any way, is "…" | The "…" balloon |

**The rules the pass keeps:**
1. **Nothing a direction carried for the game is lost.** A puzzle fact, a clue, or a war contradiction that only a direction said moves into a line someone speaks. Where it cannot, the row says so and stops for Tyler.
2. **Thad is never sarcastic** (doc 99). A narrator's dry aside does not become Thad's; it is cut, or given to someone whose voice it is.
3. **The load-bearing and the silent stay as they are** (invariants 3 and 7). HOB_C1 option 4 gets no line, no pose and no pause it did not have; its long look becomes the same "…" every pause is.
4. **Nothing the plate does not show**, and no new art in this pass. A pose that is needed goes on the image list.
5. **A sample first.** Act I, from the swindle to the claim, is the sample. No other room changes until Tyler has played it.
