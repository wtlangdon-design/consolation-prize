# THE THREE TIERS

Resolution runs most specific first.

1. **Authored pair** — this item on this target. Includes every combination that does something, and a small number that do nothing and are funny.
2. **Item pool** — this item on anything unhandled. Rotates. **This is where most of the comedy lives**, because a pool is a character's relationship to the world rather than to one object.
3. **Global pool** — anything on anything unhandled. Rotates.

**Do not add pairs merely to avoid a pool.** The fork pool is funnier than forty fork pairs would be.

# 2 · ITEM POOLS

## THE TUNING FORK — *the largest pool in the game, and it should be*

Thad strikes the fork against things. It is his whole character in one gesture, and the player will do it constantly.

> "I've struck it against it. The fork is still a perfect A. The world remains slightly out of tune."
> "It rings beautifully. Nothing else does, but give it time."
> "That can't be tuned. I've established this experimentally, which tells you something about my week."
> "A. Four hundred and forty. Everything else remains as it was, which is its loss."
> "Nothing. I am beginning to think the fault is not with the fork."

## HORSE BLANKET
> "It's a blanket. It goes over things, or under them, and that's the whole of its range."
> "I've put a blanket on it. Nothing's improved, though it does look cosier."
> "No. Even I can see that, and I'm an optimist."

## THE COMPANY MAP
> "It's a map. It tells me where things are. It doesn't do anything to them."
> "I've held a map against it. We're both embarrassed."

## THE DEED
> "I'm not going to wave a legal document at it. It's far too precious."
> "It's a paper saying that forty feet of mud belongs to me. It has no further powers, and I'm keeping an open mind."

## THE LETTER
> "It's sealed, and it's addressed to a dead man. I'm not opening it for this."

## FOUR DOLLARS
> "That's money, and that isn't a thing money buys."
> "I have four dollars, and they have a future. It isn't that."

## THE FILING FEE
*Its own pool from errata 127 (doc 94): it had shared FOUR DOLLARS', and said "I have four dollars" while Thad held two.*
> "That's the filing fee. It has one job, and this isn't it."
> "Two dollars, promised to a number in a book. I don't break promises to books."

## THE PICKAXE
> "I could. I have thought about it. I am not going to."
> "That would be destruction of property, and at least a third of the property is mine."

# 4 · AUTHORED PAIRS THAT DO NOTHING

A small number, because the joke is specific. **Room 2 only** — other rooms get theirs when composed.

> **FORK on THE POSTED NOTICES** — "Paper. It absorbs. I have learned nothing and I am going to do it again."
> **FORK on THE MUD** — "Nothing. Mud does not resonate. I have now established that experimentally and I would like it forgotten."
> **FORK on THE DOG** — "He has heard it. He has decided against having an opinion."
> **FORK on THE CHURCH STEEPLE** — "Four hundred yards away, and I have struck a tuning fork at it. I am aware."
> **FORK on THE IMPROVEMENT COMPANY SIGN** — "Gilt over pine. It sounds like pine."
> **BLANKET on THE DOG** — "He is warmer than I am."
> **MAP on THE HILLS** — "I have compared the map to the hills. The hills are winning."
> **DEED on THE MUD** — "I have shown the mud its deed. The mud is unmoved, which is the correct response and I resent it."

## Notes

1. **The fork pool is the single highest-traffic piece of writing in Act I.** The fork never leaves inventory, striking it against things is what the character does, and the player will discover within minutes that it always answers. Five variants, rotating, never consecutive. Add more as rooms are built rather than adding pairs.
2. **"I am beginning to think the fault is not with the fork"** is the closest Thad comes to self-knowledge in Act I. It should stay buried in a rotation.
3. **The gilt sign pair — "Gilt over pine. It sounds like pine."** — is the LISTEN verb's whole thesis stated as a joke about an item, twelve hours before it matters.
4. **No authored pair may exist without a written line.** If a combination should do something and no line exists, it goes in doc 15 as unwritten and the check reports it. Do not fall through to a pool to hide a gap.
