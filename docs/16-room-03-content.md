## Notes

1. **The portrait's third LOOK** puts the stock photograph in a third location — photographer's window, the Clarion, and now above the saloon. Thad notices and declines to raise it. The player has now seen the same man three times before anyone says the word "fraud."
2. **The back room's third LISTEN — "A pencil. Somebody is writing something down."** That is the judges' pencil on its string, heard through a door in Act I, fifteen hours before Thad ever enters that room to lose a duel.
3. **The cards** are a small unsettling thread: a very good hand, abandoned, never claimed. It rhymes with the nineteen names without ever touching them.
4. **The stove's third LISTEN reverses the first two.** It has gone out. Nobody has noticed. Do not "correct" this to match variants 1 and 2.
