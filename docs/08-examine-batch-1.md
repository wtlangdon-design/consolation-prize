# CONSOLATION PRIZE
## Examine Layer — Batch One

*Eight of the twenty-three manifest rooms, fully scripted. ~165 lines. Calibrated against the twelve rooms in the Examine Layer document.*

**Contains:** the second of the three load-bearing LISTEN lines (Room 8), the largest room in the game (Room 12), and the ending (Room 36).

---

## ROOM 8 — REGISTRAR'S ARCHIVE
*Entered once, in Act III, as one's own executor. Nine hotspots. Contains the timesheet.*

**THE SHELVES**
> **LOOK:** "Every claim ever filed in this district, in boxes, in order. It is the most organised thing in Consolation and no one is permitted to look at it."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Paper does not keep secrets so much as decline to volunteer."

**CLAIM ONE'S BOX**
> **LOOK:** "The most famous claim in the territory. The box is the same size as all the others, which I find unreasonable."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE FILING ITSELF**
> **LOOK:** "One sheet. A date, a name, an assertion, and a signature. There is no location on it. There has never been a location on it. Ten thousand men have dug for twelve years on the strength of a sheet of paper that does not say where."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing at all."

**THE TIMESHEET** — ***load-bearing***
> **LOOK:** "The watchman's hours, signed weekly. Twelve years of them, in a stack."
> **LISTEN:** "Paper. Same hand as the filing."

**THE EXECUTORS' LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "A record of every man who has entered this room. Nineteen names. I am the twentieth, and I am dead."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE BARRED WINDOW**
> **LOOK:** "Barred, on the inside. Whoever built this was not worried about people getting in."
> **LISTEN:** "Main Street. Muffled. It sounds like a town from in here."

**THE DUST**
> **LOOK:** "Undisturbed everywhere except one shelf, at chest height, where somebody has been reaching for years."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE LAMP** · **THE CHAIR**
> *(Chair — **LOOK:** "One chair. Facing the shelves. Somebody sits in here." **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It has been sat in a great deal and it is not saying by whom.")*

## ROOM 12 — THE MERCANTILE
*Sixteen hotspots. The highest density of jokes in the game, and Act I's shopping trip runs through all of them.*

**THE PRICE LIST**
> **LOOK:** "Flour, twelve dollars the barrel. In Providence it is two. I have read this list four times looking for the error and the error is that I came here."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is entirely comfortable with itself."

**THE SHOVELS**
> **LOOK:** "Forty shovels. There are two thousand men in Consolation and forty shovels, and somehow this has never become a problem, which tells you something about the men."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE PANS**
> **LOOK:** "Gold pans, stacked to the ceiling. He has never had to reorder."
> **LISTEN:** "They ring, faintly, when the door opens. Tin. Flat, obviously."

**THE PICKAXE**
> **LOOK:** "A pickaxe with a loose head, marked down accordingly. It is the only honest transaction available in this building."
> **LISTEN:** "The head, shifting. It has an opinion about being carried."

**THE UNSOLD SCALES**
> **LOOK:** "A dozen assayer's scales, still in their paper. He ordered them in the first year, for all the men who would need to weigh things."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. They have never been out of the paper."

**THE DYNAMITE**
> **LOOK:** "A crate of dynamite, kept beside the stove, which I have decided not to think about."
> **LISTEN:** "…No. I would rather not."

**THE CATALOGUE — cover**
> **LOOK:** "A mail-order catalogue, four hundred pages, six months old. Every man in Consolation has read it cover to cover. It is the most-read book in the territory and it is a list of prices in Chicago."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE CATALOGUE — furniture pages**
> **LOOK:** "Parlour suites. Somebody has turned down the corner of this page. Several somebodies. The corner is soft."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE CATALOGUE — the page on pianos**
> **LOOK:** "Uprights, from ninety dollars. Delivery not available west of St. Louis."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It's a picture of a piano. I am aware that I am doing this."

**THE DEBT LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "Every man in town, and a number beside him. The numbers only go one way. He is not worried, because they cannot leave until they pay, and they cannot pay."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE BOOTS** · **THE TINNED GOODS** · **THE BOLTS OF CLOTH** · **THE NAILS** · **THE LAMP OIL** · **THE STOVE**
> *(Tinned goods — **LOOK:** "Peaches, from Baltimore, at a dollar the tin. There is a man in the corner looking at them the way other men look at gold.")*
> *(Cloth — **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is the softest thing within two hundred miles and nobody in here has touched it.")*

## ROOM 19 — THAD'S HOTEL ROOM
*Nine hotspots. The letters home live here. Available in every act, and every hotspot has an act variant.*

**THE DESK**
> **LOOK:** "A desk, a chair, and a window facing an alley. For four dollars a week, I had expected a slightly worse alley."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is the only place in Consolation where nobody is talking."

**HIS MOTHER'S LETTERS**
> **LOOK Act I:** "Three from home. She is proud of me. She has been given no reason to be."
> **LOOK Act II:** "Four from home. She has told the neighbours. The neighbours are also proud of me."
> **LOOK Act III:** "Five from home. She asks whether I am eating."
> **LOOK Act IV:** "Six. I am going to answer this one properly."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Paper is very quiet about this sort of thing."

**THE OUTGOING LETTER**
> **LOOK:** *(reflects whichever version the player last chose, quoted back at them without comment)*
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE TUNING FORK CASE**
> **LOOK:** "Velvet-lined, and worth more than the fork. My father's idea of a sensible investment."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing, closed. It is a very good case."

**THE WINDOW**
> **LOOK Acts I–III:** "The alley. A cat, some barrels, and a view of the back of the Improvement Company."
> **LOOK Act IV:** "The alley. The cat has gone. The barrels are the same."
> **LISTEN:** "Somebody unloading, two streets over. It goes on all night. I have stopped minding it, which worries me."

**THE BED** · **THE WASHSTAND** · **THE COAT ON THE DOOR** · **THE WALL**
> *(The coat — **LOOK Act I:** "My good coat. It is the only thing I own that suggests I was once somebody's son with prospects." **LOOK Act III:** "My good coat. It has been in a grave.")*
> *(The wall — **LISTEN:** "Two men, next door, discussing where Mott's strike is. They are very sure. They are sure in completely different directions.")*

## ROOM 6 — ASSAY OFFICE, RECORDS ROOM
*Eleven hotspots. The nineteen names are here, and they are findable before Winnie ever mentions them.*

**THE SAMPLE WALL**
> **LOOK:** "Every sample assayed in nine years, in labelled vials. It is beautiful, in the way that only a completed thing is beautiful."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is nine years of work and it is not asking for anything."

**THE OFFICIAL LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "Nine years of assays, in a hand that has not varied once. Not one crossing-out."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE FLOORBOARD, LIFTED** *(after C5)*
> **LOOK:** "A space under a board, and a second book in it, and the second book is a different kind of document entirely."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE SECOND LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "Nineteen names. Beside each, a date, a question, and nothing else. The questions are all the same question."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing at all."

**THE NAMES — individually examinable, nineteen of them**
> **LOOK:** *(each gives a name, a trade, and a date. Nine of them will later be found on markers at Boot Hill. Ten will not, which is worse.)*
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE STOVE** · **THE COT IN THE CORNER** · **THE SPARE APRON** · **THE HIGH WINDOW** · **THE SHELF OF REFERENCE VOLUMES** · **HER TEA THINGS**
> *(The cot — **LOOK:** "A cot. Used. She has slept in this building, and recently, and more than once." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*

## ROOM 28 — SHENG'S LAUNDRY
*Twelve hotspots. Contains the stock photograph, in the window, advertising collars.*

**THE WINDOW ADVERTISEMENT**
> **LOOK:** "A portrait of a gentleman in an excellent collar, advertising the excellence of the collar. I have seen this man before. He is on the front page of the Clarion once a month and his name there is Obadiah Mott."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE COPPERS**
> **LOOK:** "Four coppers, steaming, and a system for moving between them that I could not describe but which is clearly correct."
> **LISTEN:** "Water, and a rhythm to it. Somebody has worked out the fastest way to do this and has been doing it that way for years."

**THE LEDGER**
> **LOOK:** "Laundry accounts. And, in the same book, in the same hand, in a column headed nothing at all, rather larger numbers."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE COLLARS**
> **LOOK:** "Two hundred collars, boxed, by name. Every important man in Consolation is in this box."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Starch is silent."

**THE THURSDAY BOOK**
> **LOOK:** "A separate book for Thursdays. Thursdays appear to be different."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE LINE** · **THE IRONS** · **THE BACK ROOM** · **THE STOVE** · **THE CHAIR BY THE DOOR** · **THE SCALE** · **THE STREET WINDOW**
> *(The chair — **LOOK:** "A chair by the door, facing out, worn smooth. He sits here and watches the street and everyone assumes he is resting." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*

## ROOM 27 — ABANDONED CLAIM ROW
*Eleven markers. Every one of them was somebody's certainty.*

**THE MARKERS** — *eleven, individually examinable*
> **LOOK:** *(each gives a claim name. In order along the row: **Sure Thing** · **Providence** · **The Widow's Mite** · **Second Chance** · **Last Chance** · **Katherine** · **Bonanza No. 2** · **Bonanza No. 3** · **Repentance** · **Home** · and one with the name weathered off entirely.)*
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing." *(All eleven. The same word, eleven times, and the eleventh lands differently than the first.)*

**THE WORKINGS**
> **LOOK:** "Eleven holes. Some of them are forty feet deep. Somebody dug forty feet by hand and then stopped and walked away, and I would like to know on which day he decided."
> **LISTEN:** "Wind, in the holes. It's a low sound. It is almost the same sound as my hole and it is not the same sound at all."

**THE SLUICE FRAMES** · **A BOOT** · **THE PATH**
> *(The boot — **LOOK:** "One boot. Good quality, once. It is not paired with anything." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*
> *(The path — **LOOK:** "A path, worn deep, between eleven claims that produced nothing. Somebody walked this every day for a long time." **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Nobody comes up here.")*

## ROOM 30 — MOTT'S SUPPOSED SITE
*Seven hotspots. The most-dug ground in the territory. Nothing has ever been in it.*

**THE GROUND**
> **LOOK:** "Turned over so many times that it is no longer ground so much as an opinion about where ground used to be."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Nothing whatsoever. It is the emptiest sound I have heard since I arrived."

**THE PITS**
> **LOOK:** "Perhaps four hundred separate diggings, overlapping. Ten thousand men have come here, individually, each certain that the others had looked in the wrong place."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE OFFERINGS**
> **LOOK:** "Coins, pressed into the dirt at the centre. Men have been leaving money here. I do not know what for and I do not think they do."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**A NEW DIGGING**
> **LOOK:** "Fresh. Somebody was here this week, and dug, in ground that has been dug four hundred times, and went home."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE VIEW BACK TO TOWN** · **THE SPOIL HEAPS** · **THE MARKER STONE**
> *(The marker stone — **LOOK:** "A stone, with MOTT cut into it by somebody who cared. It is not a claim marker. It is not anything. Somebody carved it because there was nothing here to look at." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*

## ROOM 36 — MAIN STREET, DAWN
*The closing screen. Room 2's nine hotspots, rewritten. The player has read the originals two hundred times.*

**THE FALSE FRONTS**
> **LOOK:** "Two storeys tall in front and one storey deep. It is still a stage set. It is just that people live here."
> **LISTEN:** "Wind. There is still a great deal of nothing behind these. It has stopped being funny and started being a fact about somebody's home."

**THE IMPROVEMENT COMPANY SIGN**
> **LOOK:** "Somebody has taken the gilt lettering down. They did it in the night. It is the most honest thing that company has ever done."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE WATER TROUGH**
> **LOOK:** "Somebody has cleaned it. It is still three inches of water, and it is clean water."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing living in it. I am unexpectedly sorry about this."

**THE BOARDWALK**
> **LOOK:** "The loose board, third from the end, has been fixed. I don't know who did it and I am not going to ask, because I would like to keep this."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It doesn't move."

**THE POSTED NOTICES**
> **LOOK:** "Claims for sale, claims disputed, and a new one: CLAIM ONE, FILED, O. MOTT, WORKINGS TO COMMENCE. The lost dog notice has been taken down." *(Conditional on the player having resolved it. If they did not: "The lost dog notice is still up.")*
> **LISTEN:** "Paper, in wind. Less of it."

**THE CHURCH STEEPLE**
> **LOOK:** "The tallest thing in Consolation, and the only building as deep as it is tall."
> **LISTEN:** "The bell is still a half-step flat. Some things are not mine to fix."

**THE DOG**
> **LOOK:** *(if resolved)* "Bill. He is sitting with somebody now. He does not appear to have noticed a difference." *(if not)* "The dog. Still nobody's."
> **LISTEN:** "Breathing. Contented. He remains the only solvent resident of Consolation."

**THE MUD**
> **LOOK:** "The same mud. I know it now."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It never had anything to say and it never pretended otherwise, and in this town that made it remarkable."

**THE HILLS**
> **LOOK:** "Gold country. For the first time since I arrived, that is simply a true statement."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. They are extremely large, and they are not doing anything, and there is gold in them."
