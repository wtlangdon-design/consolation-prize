## ROOM 43 — LOWER STREET
*Six subjects. The doors and the ways out are exits.*

**THE STREET**
> **LOOK:** "Main Street's mud, continued. Out here it has stopped trying to impress anyone."
> **LISTEN:** "Quieter than Main Street. The piano is a rumour from here."

**SUMP'S WINDOW**
> **LOOK:** "The Claims Registrar's window. A lamp, a counter, and behind it a man who has never once been surprised."
> **LISTEN:** "A pen, scratching, then a long pause, then a stamp. The rhythm of a form being found incomplete."

**THE CHURCH**
> **LOOK:** "The church, which on weekdays is the meeting hall and on most days is neither. The whitest thing in town, because it is the only thing anyone repaints."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing from inside. The pews are holding their breath for Sunday."

**THE BELL**
> **LOOK:** "A hand bell on a post, with a dent where the mallet lands. It is the town's one public note, and it is flat."
> **LISTEN:** "It hums when the wind catches it. Even humming, it's low."

**THE LIVERY**
> **LOOK:** "The livery stable. The doors stand open on lamplight, hay and the back end of a horse."
> **LISTEN:** "Horses, shifting their weight and their opinions."

**THE TROUGH**
> **LOOK:** "A water trough with a skin of dust on it and the moon in the middle."
> **LISTEN:** "A slow drip from the seam, keeping its own time."

## ROOM 44 — THE LANE
*Six subjects. The doors and the ways out are exits.*

**THE LANE ITSELF**
> **LOOK:** "The Lane: the street the town keeps for the businesses it doesn't put on the main one."
> **LISTEN:** "Main Street at one end, Lower Street at the other, and in between a hush you could rent."

**THE COFFIN BY THE DOOR**
> **LOOK:** "A pine coffin standing on end by the undertaker's door, like a man waiting to be let in."
> **LISTEN:** "Hollow. A good coffin rings like a cello, and this one rings like a cheap cello."

**THE PHOTOGRAPHER'S WINDOW**
> **LOOK:** "The photographer's window. In the middle of it, a portrait of a distinguished gentleman in a very good collar, lit like a saint."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. The gentleman in the window has been holding very still for a long time."

**OZYMANDIA'S TENT**
> **LOOK:** "Madame Ozymandia's tent, painted with more stars than the sky can spare."
> **LISTEN:** "Humming, low and long, from inside. Either a lady concentrating or a lady charging."

**THE BARBER'S POLE**
> **LOOK:** "A barber's pole, painted to look as though it turns. It doesn't. That is the first lie a barber tells you."
> **LISTEN:** "The pole creaking in its bracket. It would turn if it could."

**THE EMPTY STOREFRONT**
> **LOOK:** "An empty storefront, boarded, its sign painted out. Somebody's whole idea, closed for good."
> **LISTEN:** "Mice, running the business now."
