This document does, with a count and a target for each, and `tools/check-pacing.mjs` now holds the ones that can be counted (`proofs/pacing.json`, `yardstick`).

It sits over doc 114. Where a scene designed there would have been a conversation, this is why it becomes an object.

## What this changes in doc 114's plan

1. **Objects first.** Every scene still to be designed asks, before anything else: what does he pick up here, and what is it used on later? A scene that ends with a paper handed across a counter is looked at again.
2. **The papers stay.** The three documents and the forms are the plot, and Sump's office is a running joke about paper. The fix is more objects beside them, not fewer papers.
3. **Rooms give things up.** Each built room is gone through once for one or two things Thad can take that the room already describes: the Nugget, the livery, the Mercantile (which has three takeable things and uses one), the laundry, the church, the barber's.
4. **Told becomes acted** as pictures allow: each art brief from here on asks for the poses its scene's directions need.
