# THE LAST CLAIM IN CONSOLATION
## 92 · The act-change pass — new lines for rooms you come back to

*Tyler, 2026-11-06, on the pre-playthrough review's finding that the town looks and reads the same in every act ("some of the rooms had totally different items in them and new things could be clicked. I always thought that was a cool touch"), told that more of it needs new writing the project's rules forbid: **"I'm giving you permission to do it."** Errata 113 records the ruling.*

## The rules of the pass

1. **What it writes:** act variants. These are the lines a hotspot answers with in a later act, so a room the player knows reads differently when they return. Doc 05 Part Two-B's doctrine still holds: a hotspot re-answers when what Thad knows changes what he sees. That is not every hotspot in every act.
2. **What it does not touch:**
   - Puzzles, flags, gates, items, hotspots, repeat counts.
   - Anything a puzzle reads.
   - The three load-bearing LISTEN lines. They are never given a variant, so no act can replace them.
   - Any LISTEN that states a fact a puzzle uses. On Main Street that is the steeple's flat bell and the boardwalk's loose board.
   - The letters home.
   - No line hints at a solution, at the gold under Prosperity, or at the filing.
3. **The picture does not change between acts.** A line never describes something the plate does not show: no crepe on doorways that are not draped. Sound, memory, what Thad now knows and how the town treats him are all fair.
4. **True whenever it can be read** (doc 59 rule 11). An act 3 line is true from the funeral to the end of Act III. An act 4 line is true before and after the Listening, so it may know that Mott invented the strike and that Hob is Mott, and never that the gold is real.
5. **Doc 59's voice.** One writer, no subagents. Written in the documents and extracted, never typed into /content.
6. **Main Street was the sample.** Tyler ruled on it on 2026-11-06, without playing it: "I trust your judgement so proceed with what you wrote" (errata 114). The pass goes on room by room, a batch to a pull request.

**The running jokes it carries:**
- The false fronts go from greeting him by name (Act II), to mourning him (Act III), to being the most honest thing on the street (Act IV).
- The hills go from "nobody's looking for me" (Act III) to "they all look so busy" (Act IV).

**Left alone on purpose:**
- The steeple and the boardwalk's LISTEN, which carry facts.
- The mud in Act IV, because Room 36's dawn mud is the payoff ("It held my boot the first night and the truth the whole time").
