## ROOM 22 — THE ROAD TO THE CLAIMS
*Five hotspots. Traversed dozens of times. The two crowd hotspots are the whole room.*

**THE MEN GOING OUT**
> **LOOK:** "Men walking out with tools over their shoulders, at six in the morning. They are talking. Several of them are talking at once."
> **LISTEN:** "Talk. A great deal of it, all at the same volume, none of it listened to. It's cheerful."

**THE MEN COMING BACK**
> **LOOK:** "The same men, walking in, at seven at night. Same tools. Same road."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Two dozen men on a road and not one of them is saying anything."

**THE MILEPOST**
> **LOOK:** "CLAIMS — 2 MI. Somebody has scratched a great many other numbers underneath, none of which agree."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE ROAD** · **THE SCRUB**
> *(The road — **LOOK:** "Worn a foot deep in twelve years. This much dirt was moved by boots, not by digging, and it is more than anybody has moved on purpose.")*

## ROOM 25 — PRATT'S CLAIM
*Eight hotspots. The best-worked claim in the district. It has never produced anything.*

**THE WORKINGS**
> **LOOK:** "Square. Timbered properly. Swept — somebody has *swept* a mine. This is the finest piece of work in the territory and there has never been a thing in it."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. It is very well made and there is nothing in it."

**THE TALLY POST**
> **LOOK:** "Days worked, cut into the post in fives. I have counted them twice. Two thousand and six."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE SPOIL HEAP**
> **LOOK:** "Waste rock, sorted by size. He has sorted his waste rock. Nobody asked him to and nobody will ever look at it."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE TOOL RACK**
> **LOOK:** "Tools, hung, oiled, in order of length. Mine are in a pile and one of them is a raccoon's now."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE LEAN-TO**
> **LOOK:** "He sleeps out here. There is a bed, a stove, a cup, and a shelf, and the shelf is level."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE SIGN** · **THE SLUICE** · **THE BOUNDARY**
> *(The sign — **LOOK:** "C. PRATT. No claim name. Every other man out here named his. He has just put his name on it, which on reflection is the only accurate sign in the district.")*

## ROOM 26 — THE CREEK & SLUICE
*Seven hotspots.*

**THE CREEK**
> **LOOK:** "Six feet across and eight inches deep, and the reason two thousand people live here."
> **LISTEN:** "Water. It is the only thing in this territory that is doing exactly what it appears to be doing."

**THE SLUICE BOXES**
> **LOOK:** "Forty sluices along a hundred yards of creek, each one built to catch what the one above it missed. The men at the bottom are working the leavings of the men at the top, and the men at the top have found nothing."
> **LISTEN:** "Water, through wood, forty times over. It's almost a chord. It is not quite a chord."

**THE RIFFLES**
> **LOOK:** "Cleats to catch the heavy material. There is a great deal of heavy material and none of it is gold."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE MAN PANNING**
> **LOOK:** "A man, crouched, doing the same motion he was doing when I passed this morning."
> **LISTEN:** "Water, and breathing, and nothing else at all. He has not spoken in nine hours."

**THE BANK** · **THE DISCARDED PAN** · **THE FAR SIDE**
> *(The discarded pan — **LOOK:** "A gold pan, in the willows, thrown. Not dropped. Thrown." **LISTEN:** "Nothing.")*

## ROOM 29 — THE HIGH RIDGE
*Five hotspots. The whole town, from above, looking small and painted.*

**CONSOLATION, BELOW**
> **LOOK:** "The town, from above. From here you can see that every building on Main Street is a front with nothing behind it, and that people are moving between them anyway, briskly, all day."
> **LISTEN:** "Faintly, all of it at once. It sounds exactly like a real town."

**THE FOUNDATIONS**
> **LOOK:** "From up here you can see the order they were built in. The saloon first. The assay office second. The schoolhouse third — before the church, before the bank, before anybody's house. They built a school for children who had not arrived yet, and no teacher ever came."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE CLAIMS**
> **LOOK:** "Four hundred claims, laid out below like a badly ruled page. Somewhere in the middle is forty feet that belongs to me."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Four hundred holes and not a sound out of any of them."

**THE ROAD EAST** · **THE WIND**
> *(The road east — **LOOK:** "Eleven hundred miles that way. From up here you can see about nine of them." **LISTEN:** "Nothing coming." — *the same line as the opening screen, and the player has heard it before.*)*

## ROOM 31 — THE FUNERAL
*Nine hotspots. Thad is present at his own, and can examine all of it, which is the joke and also not the joke.*

**THE MOURNERS**
> **LOOK:** "Nine people have come to my funeral. I have been in Consolation eleven weeks. I do not know what to do with this and I am going to have to get into the box shortly."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing. Nine people, not talking."

**HOB, AT THE EDGE**
> **LOOK:** "The night watchman, standing at the back, in daylight. I have never seen him in daylight."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE MARKER**
> **LOOK:** "THADEUS GRUBBE. Frame supplied the spelling. It is the last one and it is not the best one."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE COFFIN**
> **LOOK:** "Plain. Not Respectable, and certainly not Handsome. It is going to be very dark in there."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**REVEREND SOWERBY**
> **LOOK:** "He is preparing to deliver four words for fifty cents and he is preparing thoroughly."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing yet."

**THE DIRT PILE**
> **LOOK:** "The dirt that was in the hole, beside the hole, waiting."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE HATS** · **THE SKY** · **BOOT HILL, BEYOND**
> *(The hats — **LOOK:** "Every man here has taken his hat off. For me. On the strength of a notice in the Clarion that misspelled my name.")*

## ROOM 34 — UNDER PROSPERITY
*Eight hotspots. LOOK is beginning to run out; LISTEN is beginning to take over. This room is the transition into the listening chamber.*

**THE TUNNEL**
> **LOOK:** "Forty feet of my own digging. It is not square and it is not swept and Nickel Pratt would weep."
> **LISTEN:** "Air. Still moving. Still going somewhere."

**THE ROCK FACE**
> **LOOK:** "Rock."
> **LISTEN:** "…Different. Not by much."

**THE DRAUGHT**
> **LOOK:** "Nothing to look at. There has never been anything to look at."
> **LISTEN:** "Warmer. It has been warmer every foot."

**THE LAMP**
> **LOOK:** "One lamp, and about four hours of oil in it."
> **LISTEN:** "Burning."

**THE TUNING FORK**
> **LOOK:** "My father thought this was a sensible investment. He was, on the evidence, the only person in my family who has ever been right about anything."
> **LISTEN:** "A. Four hundred and forty. It is still the only correct thing within a hundred miles."

**THE WAY BACK** · **THE FLOOR** · **THE TIMBERING**
> *(The way back — **LOOK:** "Daylight, a long way up, the size of a coin." **LISTEN:** "The town. Very faint. Somebody is arguing about a boundary.")*

## ROOM 35 — THE COMPANY VAULT
*Ten hotspots. Everything Fanshawe has been protecting, and none of it is gold.*

**THE ORIGINAL SUBSCRIPTION**
> **LOOK:** "The subscription for the Mott expedition, Boston, 1847. Eleven names. The fourth is A. Fanshawe. He was not a colonel then either."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE MAPS**
> **LOOK:** "The originals of all eleven maps to Mott's strike. Same hand. Same paper. Same ink. They were drawn in an afternoon."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE STOCK PORTRAIT BOXES**
> **LOOK:** "Four gross of portraits of a man in a good collar, purchased at wholesale in 1851. The Clarion has been printing him as Obadiah Mott for eleven years."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE REAL LEDGERS**
> **LOOK:** "The Company's actual accounts. It has never once traded in gold. It trades in maps, permits, tools, filings, lots, lumber and hope, and it has been extraordinarily profitable."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE UNSOLD DEEDS**
> **LOOK:** "Two hundred claims, unsold, held quietly. All of them adjoining Claim One. Every piece of ground a man might dig near the strike belongs to the Company, and has for years."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing."

**THE STRONGBOX**
> **LOOK:** "The strongbox. Open. Empty. There has never been gold in this building, and the man who runs it has known that longer than anyone alive."
> **LISTEN:** "Nothing at all."

**THE CORRESPONDENCE FILE** · **THE SHARE CERTIFICATES** · **THE LAMP** · **THE DOOR**
> *(The correspondence file — **LOOK:** "Letters to Boston. Nine years of them, copied out in his own hand, all saying the same thing in different words: that the search continues, that hopes are high, and that further funds would be timely.")*

## Two amendments to back-propagate

1. **Fanshawe has never entered the Liar's Assay** (Room 4 tally board). Duel Four is the first of his life.
2. **The schoolhouse was the third building in Consolation** (Room 29 foundations), before the church and the bank. This is now stated twice — once from the ridge, once in the schoolhouse itself — and nowhere else.
