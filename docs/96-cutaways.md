# 96 · The cut-aways

*Tyler, 2026-11-07, asking whether the game had room for Monkey Island's cut-aways ("like when LeChuck is in the underworld"): offered three, he answered "You decide, and I want them included now so that they're part of my play through." Errata 158 records the ruling and the permission for the new lines, which are written here, in one voice, under doc 59.*

A cut-away leaves Thad where he is and shows somewhere else. The screen fades to black and comes up on the other room with nobody's hand on the controls. The lines are said, the screen fades again, and the game comes back exactly where it was. Each plays once, at the first quiet moment after its point in the story: no conversation open, no scene running, nothing waiting to be said, and Thad in some other room.

**The rules these keep:**
- **No hint, ever.** A cut-away never says what to do or how a puzzle is solved (invariant 4). The Pratt scene names no money and no amount, because D5 is solved by working out how he is paid.
- **No state.** Watching one changes nothing in the story but its own once-only flag. Nothing is set by walking into its room, nobody accosts, nothing is said on arrival, and nothing is saved there.
- **Hob is never in one.** His identity is the game's best reveal.
- **Fanshawe appears only after the end of Part Two.** Before that, the player is meant to take three or four conversations to notice him (doc 01, the Fanshawe rule).
- **True whenever it plays.** No line assumes a choice the player might not have made.
