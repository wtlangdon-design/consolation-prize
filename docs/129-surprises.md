# PART ONE — WHAT A SURPRISE IS

A **surprise** is a click whose result the player could not have predicted from the click, and which is funny. It is not a puzzle and it does not help: it never points at a solution, never changes a flag and never marks anything (invariants 1, 3, 4 and 9). Monkey Island is full of them, and they are much of what players remember.

The kinds, with the best of each the game already has:

| Kind | What happens | Ours already |
|---|---|---|
| **The switch** | The menu offers a bold line; the hero says something else | None in a conversation. The letters home, where "flattering, with a number in it" writes a letter nobody would expect, are the nearest |
| **The escalation** | A try fails, and fails in a new way that makes it worse | The raccoon: bait brings three more raccoons; the egg goes in and comes back out, at speed |
| **The world answers back** | Something that should not respond does | The wardrobe, locked: "I heard it try the door a moment ago, from the inside, and give up." The glass at Fanshawe's table, refilled before it reaches the cloth |
| **The off-route find** | A thing nobody asked for, and the game noticed | Dressing the raccoon (button, collar, parsley); the B on the coffin; the barber's chair lever |
| **The long payoff** | Something planted hours earlier comes back | The misspellings of his name, spelled right at last on a headstone for a man who is not in the grave; Bill, the lost dog, who was on Main Street the whole time |
| **The genre joke** | A joke on what this kind of game does | Thad attends his own funeral, in a game where you cannot die. The Liars' Assay is the whole town doing insult duels as a local custom |

**Why the switch is ours.** Doc 99 says Thad is courteous to everything and "would apologise to a door". The menu offers the rude line a player picks *because* it is rude (doc 99 rule 3), and Thad cannot bring himself to say it. So the switch is not a borrowed gag: it is his character, played as a joke on the player's choice.

# PART THREE — THE PASS

## The rules

1. **One or two a room, never more.** A room of surprises is a room of noise.
2. **The switch: a person has at most one,** and Thad's save is something he could say to whoever it is, at that point in the story: a stranger gets good wishes, not a party (errata 305).
   The switch and only on a `[COMIC]` option, so the menu can never hide what a choice does. Thad starts the bold line and saves himself: a word cut off and replaced ("a cr— a credit") or a sentence rescued by its ending ("You're never leaving this town… without a proper send-off, I mean!"). The other person answers the line he *nearly* said, in their own voice.
3. **Nothing a surprise touches carries the game.** No flag, puzzle, clue, act truth or LISTEN line (invariants 3 and 9, doc 92). A surprise never points at a solution.
4. **Nothing the plate does not show,** and no new art. A surprise is told in what people say or in a pose a figure already has. A direction says what is seen, never what someone feels (errata 294).
5. **True whenever it is read,** in every act the room is visited.
6. **One voice,** under doc 99 and doc 59: Thad warm, flourish first and an honest beat last, never sarcastic.

## Main Street, the sample

Three switches, one for each of the street's people who talks with Thad at length. Each is a follow-up, so it is offered only after the conversation has given Thad a reason to be bold, and each is in doc 07 under its person.

| Who | The menu offers | Thad says | They answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| The pie woman, after "What's in the pie?" | "Madam, that pie is a crime against the territory." | "Madam, that pie is a cr— a credit to the territory! A credit. I'd say so in front of anybody." | "Insults are a penny extra. You very nearly bought one." |
| The map seller, after "Is that a map to Mott's strike?" | "Your maps are a swindle, sir, and I'll tell the whole town so!" | "Your maps are a sw— a wonder, sir! A wonder, and I'll tell the whole town so!" | "Tell them, sir! I'll draw you on the next one, telling them." — "Me?" — "Little hat. Arm out. Top of the hill, beside Pike." |
| The man leaving tomorrow, after "When are you leaving?" | "You're never leaving this town, are you?" | "You're never leaving this town… without my very best wishes, I mean! There. You have them." | "Keep them. You'll need them more." |

The pie woman answers in money, as she answers everything. The map seller takes it as the best news of his week and adds a detail nobody asked for (doc 99 rule 4). The man leaving tomorrow hands Thad's good wishes straight back: a newcomer will need them more (errata 305). *It was a send-off, "I'll see to it myself"; Tyler, 2026-11-14: "it seems odd that Thad would immediately offer to throw him a send off within minutes of entering Consolation for the first time". A save has to be something a stranger could offer.*
