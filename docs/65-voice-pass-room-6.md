# THE LAST CLAIM IN CONSOLATION
## 65 · The voice pass, Room 6 — before and after

*Errata 100. Winnie Ledger's records room, behind the counter. A light pass: these lines carry the room's quiet on purpose, and doc 59 keeps sadness where it belongs. Contractions, fewer bare "Nothing." openings, and the stool that announces whoever sits on it, now announcing Thad at the window too. Changed in docs 08, 49 and 54 and re-extracted. Unchanged: every puzzle fact, every flag and gate.*
