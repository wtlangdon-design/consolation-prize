# 105 · The six interiors — the lines

*Tyler, 2026-11-08, on the written-versus-built audit's first gap (doc 104): "Build all six". Told that only eight of their objects had lines and that building them meant writing about 250: "Write all six now". That is the fifth writing exception (errata 183), and these lines are written under it, in one voice, under doc 59 and doc 99. The rules:*
- *Thad is sincere and wants things. He hears what is wrong with everything, and he is a little proud of it.*
- *Every line is true whenever it is read. The rooms are open in every act, at night like the streets they stand on (errata 183). No line assumes a day, a meeting, a puzzle or an edition of the Clarion.*
- *Nothing the room does not show. The art brief (`reference/ancillary-interiors/brief.md`) draws what these lines describe and nothing they do not.*
- *No hint, nothing marked, nothing cycling for a reason (invariants 3 and 9). The stock portraits are what doc 07 already says they are, and no more.*
- *Doc 07's written lines are kept word for word as variant 1. Its people (the barber, the photographer, the two boarding-house women) keep their trees and barks as doc 07 writes them.*

*Room numbers: doc 12 numbered the six 36 to 41, before Room 36 became Main Street at dawn. Errata 183 renumbers them 37 to 42: the parlour 37, the studio 38, the bakehouse 39, the storefront 40, the schoolhouse 41, the barber's 42.*
