Diagnostics

A per-note-class offset of −100 cents on pitch class 1, in every octave, summed with the global detune rather than replacing it. Toggle it while the theme is playing — the melody lands on C♯ in bars 3, 6 and on the held final note of bar 8.

Audition figures at the current global detune. Not content — they exist so each synth voice can be judged on its own. Harmonica does not appear in the theme: doc 28 scores CONSOLATION for piano, fiddle and jaw harp.

What this is claiming

  • Melody is placeholder, written against doc 28's description (cheerful, slightly too fast, cadence unresolved so it goes round again rather than ending). It ends on the leading tone and loops. Replace it freely.
  • D major, so C♯ is the leading tone — the most conspicuous note in the key to be flat by a semitone.
  • The A7 chords are voiced without their third. With a flat C♯ in every dominant chord the theme stops being uncomfortable and starts being broken. The C♯s that matter are in the melody, where they are heard.
  • Output stage quantises to 10 bits and rolls off at 11 kHz on purpose. 1990, not a sample library.
