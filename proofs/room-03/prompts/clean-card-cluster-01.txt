ONE COHERENT CARD-TABLE COMPOSITION for a 2D point-and-click adventure game in the
tradition of The Secret of Monkey Island. Stylised early-1990s graphic-adventure
pixel art at a modern source resolution. NOT photorealistic, NOT PBR, NOT glossy 3D,
NOT AAA concept art, NOT smooth modern character illustration, NOT portrait rendering.

SUBJECT. A round card table in an 1858 frontier saloon at night, with FOUR seated
men playing cards, their four chairs, and a fifth place that is conspicuously EMPTY.

REFERENCE 1 IS THE STAGING AND IS BINDING. It is a grayscale blocking diagram of
exactly this composition, drawn to the game's own camera. Reproduce its geometry:
the table's size and elliptical perspective, where each man sits, which way each man
faces, who is in front of the table and who is behind it, and the gap at the near
side. Replace its grey blocks with finished art. Do not rearrange it.

THE FIVE-PLACE RING, and the two things it exists to get right.
  - The table is round, about 1.35 m across, seen from slightly above and in front.
  - FOUR men sit on it at 72 and 144 degrees either side of the camera direction.
  - THE TWO NEAR MEN (left and right, lower in the frame, drawn LARGER) are seen
    FROM BEHIND, in three-quarter back view. We see their backs, their shoulders,
    the backs of their heads and their chair backs. WE DO NOT SEE THEIR FACES.
  - THE TWO FAR MEN (higher in the frame, drawn SMALLER) face into the room and
    toward the camera. We see their faces.
  - NOT ALL FOUR FACE THE CAMERA. A composition with four faces in it is wrong and
    must be redrawn.
  - THE FIFTH PLACE, nearest the camera, is EMPTY: no chair, no man, nothing. The
    ring of men is open at the front.

THE FOUR MEN, who are specific people and not a crowd:
  1  NEAR LEFT, back three-quarter view: a man in a flat cap, work coat, holding a
     fanned hand of cards we see the back of.
  2  FAR LEFT, facing the room: grey hair and side-whiskers, an older man in a dark
     coat and waistcoat, holding a fanned hand.
  3  FAR RIGHT, facing the room: the young one, shirtsleeves and a blue neckerchief,
     holding a fanned hand.
  4  NEAR RIGHT, back three-quarter view: bald, wire spectacles, seen from behind so
     the spectacle arm shows at the side of his head, holding a fanned hand.

PHYSICAL CONTACT IS THE POINT OF THIS IMAGE. Every one of these must be true:
  - each man's hips are genuinely ON his chair seat and his weight is supported;
  - his knees and boots go UNDER or AROUND the table, on the ground, not floating;
  - the TABLE TOP CROSSES the far men's bodies at the waist -- they are behind it;
  - the near men are IN FRONT of the table and their shoulders overlap its rim;
  - forearms and hands rest ON the table surface, in contact with it, not hovering;
  - no man sits on the table, no limb passes through the table or a chair;
  - nobody's feet are in the air and nobody is standing.

ON THE TABLE: a scatter of chips and coins, a bottle, two tin cups, and -- on the
RIM AT THE EMPTY FIFTH PLACE, nearest the camera -- an ABANDONED HAND OF CARDS LYING
FACE UP, clearly belonging to nobody, with no man and no chair behind it.

BACKGROUND. FLAT SOLID MAGENTA (255, 0, 255), edge to edge, everywhere that is not
the table, the chairs, the men or what is on the table. NO floor, NO dirt, NO walls,
NO back bar, NO room, NO cast shadow on the ground, NO vignette, NO gradient, NO
texture in the magenta. The magenta is keyed out and anything drawn into it becomes
foreign scenery inside another room. This matters more than it sounds.

LIGHT. Warm interior practical light from above and slightly to the right -- an oil
chandelier and a stove out of frame. Warm ochre and umber on the lit planes, cool
umber-grey in shadow, deep values that still read at a glance. Reference 5 (the
assay office) is the visual-language authority for how light and material are drawn
in this game; match its restraint, not a modern render's.

CHARACTER RENDERING -- this is a hard gate and it has failed before.
Reference 3 is Thad, the player character, and he is THE RULER. These four men must
be drawn at HIS level of abstraction, not above it. At gameplay scale require:
  - simplified eye treatment, the same weight of mark Thad's eyes are drawn with;
  - hair, beards and side-whiskers as GROUPED MASSES, never individual strands;
  - restrained skin modelling: two or three values, no smooth cheek gradients;
  - restrained cloth folds: readable tonal masses, not rendered drapery;
  - hard, legible silhouettes that read at a glance;
  - NO portrait eyeballs, NO catchlights, NO individually drawn moustache hairs,
    NO airbrushed skin, NO rendered-illustration finish.
A man here must look like he came from the same hand and the same year as Thad. If
he looks better drawn than Thad, he is wrong.
