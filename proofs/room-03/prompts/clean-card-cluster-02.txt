THE SAME PICTURE WITH NOBODY IN IT.

Reference 1 is the image being edited. It shows a round card table in an 1858
frontier saloon at night, with four seated men, four chairs, and things on the
table, on a flat magenta field. Produce THE IDENTICAL PICTURE WITH THE FOUR MEN
AND THEIR FOUR CHAIRS REMOVED -- the table and its contents alone, five seconds
before anybody sat down.

WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE, and this is the whole operation:
  - the table's position, size, elliptical perspective, rim, apron, legs and feet;
  - the wood, its grain, its colour and its lighting;
  - the bottle, the two tin cups, every chip and every coin, in the same places;
  - THE ABANDONED HAND OF CARDS LYING FACE UP on the near rim -- it stays exactly
    where it is, face up, belonging to nobody;
  - the magenta field.
The unmasked centre of the table is held back so you can see all of this. Complete
the table outward from it; do not re-invent it.

WHAT TO REMOVE:
  - all four men, entirely -- heads, bodies, arms, hands, and the fans of cards
    they are holding;
  - all four of their chairs, entirely;
  - and nothing else.

WHAT TO DRAW WHERE THEY WERE:
  - where a man's arm, hand or cards lay ON THE TABLE: continue the TABLE. The
    wood grain, the rim, the apron and the lighting carry straight through, as
    though the arm had never been there. This is the point of the operation --
    the table has to be whole under where the arms were.
  - where a man or a chair stood against the background: FLAT SOLID MAGENTA
    (255, 0, 255), the same magenta as the rest of the field. No floor, no dirt,
    no walls, no room, no shadow on the ground, no gradient, no texture.

DO NOT add anybody. Do not add a fifth man, a barman, a hand, an arm, a hat on a
chair, or a figure in the distance. Do not add furniture. Do not add a floor. Do
not move the table. Do not re-light it. Do not change its shape.

The result is composited back against the original, pixel for pixel, and every
difference between the two is taken to be a man. Anything you add that was not
there will be taken to be a man as well.
