This is not a new room. It is the SAME room, repainted empty. Reference 1 is a
background plate from a 2D point-and-click adventure game that is already

painted into it and they must come out.

Your job is to give back that same picture -- same room, same size, same camera,
same bar, same card table, same stove, same stairs, same piano, same dirt floor,

Do not redesign anything. Do not improve anything. Do not tidy the room. Do not

THE STOVE-SIDE END OF THE BAR -- READ THIS TWICE
======================================================================

In reference 1 the far (left, stove-side) end of the counter PETERS OUT. It thins
and dissolves into the floor and wall near the stove instead of ending. That is
the one thing in reference 1 that is wrong, and it is wrong because a man was
standing in front of it hiding the problem. He is gone now, so it must be built.

Give that end a real physical resolution. Any of these is correct:
  - a proper end stile and moulded cap that closes the counter off;
  - a short return running back to the wall;
  - the counter continuing cleanly behind the stove and its pipe.

It must NOT fade out, smear into the dirt, thin to nothing, or leave the foot
rail running on into empty air. A carpenter built this bar and it has an end.
