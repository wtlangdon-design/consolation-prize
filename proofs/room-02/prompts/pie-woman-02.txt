THE ONE THING TO CHANGE — DRAWING DETAIL. Reference 2 is THAD, the protagonist of this game, at the size the game draws him. He is the target and he is not negotiable. Every figure here must look like it was drawn by the same hand, for the same game, at the same degree of simplification as him. Right now these figures are far too finely rendered beside him: they read as modern painted portraits shrunk down, and he reads as a hand-drawn 1990s adventure-game sprite. Redraw them so that is no longer true.

WHAT THAT MEANS, CONCRETELY:
- A FACE CARRIES AT MOST: the mass of the brow and hair, a shape for each eye, one shadow for the nose, one line for the mouth, one shadow under the jaw. Nothing else.
- NO eyeball modelling, NO catchlights, NO eyelid creases, NO individual eyelashes, NO nostril detail, NO teeth, NO lip modelling, NO ear cartilage.
- NO skin texture, NO pores, NO cheek blush, NO stubble rendered hair by hair. A beard is ONE mass with two or three tones, not thousands of hairs.
- HAIR IS A SHAPE, not strands.
- CLOTH IS FLAT MASSES with two or three tones and hard-edged folds. NO soft airbrushed gradients, NO fabric weave, NO thread, NO subtle sheen.
- Buttons, buckles and watch chains are single small marks or are left out. They are not jewellery.
- EDGES ARE CRISP. Where two tones meet they meet on a hard line, not through a blur.
- Fewer, larger shapes everywhere. If a detail would be smaller than an eye, it does not exist.

The test is: at gameplay size, does this face carry the same amount of information as Thad's face, and no more?
