THE MAP SELLER of Consolation — one character, alone, whole.

WHO HE IS. A hopeful man of about thirty who sells hand-drawn maps to a gold strike, on a street where nobody has found anything. He believes in the maps completely and he is wrong, and he has no idea he is being used by the company that prints them. He is the saddest man in this town and he is played entirely for laughs: eager, tidy, upright, genuinely delighted to see a customer. Nothing in his face knows what we know.

WHAT HE HAS. One folded map held up and open in his near hand, out and away from his coat, at chest height, turned so its blank back faces us — NO drawn lines, NO lettering, NO markings of any kind on it. A canvas satchel of rolled maps on the opposite hip.
