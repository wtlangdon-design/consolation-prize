**Why it is retired rather than repaired.** Tyler: *"We have spent too much time repairing
inherited furniture geometry, floor contamination, character registration, compositing seams,
halos, occlusion, scale relationships, old masks, old coordinates. STOP RETROFITTING IT."* The
lineage's root defect is structural, not cosmetic: **seven of the nine patrons are painted into
the plate**, and a painted patron can never be animated. Phase 2B needs nine independent figures,
and no amount of raster repair produces one from a baked one.

**What may still be taken from it.** Mood, canonical content, frontier vocabulary, the room's
identity, and the historical record of what failed and why. **Not pixels.** No scenery chunk from
this lineage may be pasted into the new room to close a seam — §18, and the reason is that every
such patch is a new registration dependency on geometry that is no longer canon.

**The one thing carried forward is the CAMERA**, and deliberately: the Thad depth relationship
(227 px at y 506, 526 px at y 858, a true perspective reaching zero at y 239) was measured,
cross-checked against the back door, built as an A/B/C study and **owner-accepted**. §3 says not
to canonise a scale incidentally, so the new room is designed to the same camera and every other
number is built from zero against it. `proofs/room-03/clean-sheet/blocking.json`.

**Old coordinates are not canon.** Old furniture geometry is not canon. Old masks are not canon.
Old Room 3 background pixels are not canon. The raccoon's old staging rect is not a design
constraint on the new room.
