# cast-bar-pair-01/source.png — REJECTED ON STYLE — NOT INTEGRATED

The final Phase 2A image operation Tyler authorized, spent and not used. **Neither candidate was
promoted.** `art/actors/cast-nugget-bar-2.png` and `art/actors/cast-nugget-bar-3.png` are
byte-identical to what they were, and so is every other character in the room.

## The method worked

This was the whole point of the new input topology, and it held. The source contained only the two
men, pasted at native resolution with a 340 px gap on flat magenta, and no Room 3 environment art
was transmitted. What came back was **the same two men, in the same poses, in the same clothes and
colours, holding the same objects, facing the same way, at the same places on the canvas**. Nobody
was added, nobody was deleted, no scene was composed, and neither man was turned into Thad. Every
failure of the masked family-sheet attempt is absent here.

The three-bar composition would still have read as one patron seated on a stool, one leaning on the

## Criterion B — style: BOTH FAIL

They are **better**, and that is worth saying precisely: the hair is now a mass with a hard edge
rather than strands, the bowler is a flatter shape, the moustache is one grouped mass, the cloth
carries fewer and harder folds, and the cheek has a hard-edged shadow plane where it used to have a
continuous ramp. Four of the named faults are genuinely gone.

But the test is whether the face now belongs in Thad's vocabulary at the height the room draws it,
and at 380 px and 494 px it does not:

- **The eye is still rendered as an eyeball** — a white sclera with a dark iris and a lid line, and
  on `bar_3` a visible catchlight. Thad's eye at the same height is one solid dark bar. Every
  accepted figure in the game — the pie woman, the stove man, card player 4 — has an eye that is
  one dark shape. This is the single feature that survives all other simplification and it is on
  Tyler's explicit list: *"eye as one simple readable shape per side, no eye catchlight rendering."*
- **The cheek and nose still carry smooth continuous modelling** through many intermediate tones,
  against Thad's three flat tones meeting on hard steps.

`renders/opening-set-retrofit/phase2a-pair-eyes.webp` is that comparison at 8×, and it is the thing
that decided it.

## No metric was used to decide this

Every measurement tried on this question is inert. Colour count, flatness and neighbour step all
rank **Thad** as the softer image, because his sprite is a resampled 742 px painting; on a
soft-step ratio across the head Thad scores 45.5% against the `bar_3` candidate's 39.6%, which
would say the candidate is already harder-edged than the target. It is not. The call was made by
looking, which is what Tyler asked for.

## The standing option, which costs nothing

These candidates are strictly better than the retained art on hair, hat, moustache and cloth, and
strictly worse than nothing on the eyes. They are kept here intact. **Swapping either or both of
them in requires no image operation** — only a decision to accept a better-but-still-failing
figure. That is Tyler's call and not one this pass will make on his behalf, because his instruction
was not to lower the acceptance standard.
